    Part 01 - Foundations

    Goal: own numbers, dates, clock time, and the tiny time words that change meaning. Without this, Hörens fails and small talk stops.

    Files in this Part (read in order)

    # File What you learn Time
    01 numbers-dates.md 1-1000, prices, days/months/seasons, ordinals 1-31, halb/Viertel/nach/vor, 24h clock, opening hours, spelling alphabet 60 min
    02 time-words.md noch/schon/erst/nur, noch nicht vs noch kein, immer/oft/manchmal/nie, gestern/heute/morgen, immer noch/noch mal 30 min
    03 pronunciation-spelling.md umlauts, ß, ch/sch/sp/st/z, vowel length, word stress, sentence melody - the sounds of German 45 min
    04 grammar-terms.md the grammar words used in this book (subject, object, cases, verb forms) explained in plain English 30 min

    How this Part connects

    • After this Part -> go to 02-Grammar-Core for articles and cases.
    • You will reuse this Part daily: every phone number, price, appointment, and "halb neun" uses it.
    • Practice: after reading, do 08-Practice-Lab sentences that use time and numbers.

    What you can do after this Part

    • Understand any price, time, or date you hear (Hören Part 1).
    • Say your birthday, phone number, and appointment correctly.
    • Use noch nicht / schon / erst without translating.

    Timed Practice (weekly, 15-20 min)

    1. Set a timer for 15-20 minutes.
    2. Do the Time prepositions block in 02-sentences.md section 14 (6 items).
    3. Do the Time words block in 02-sentences.md section 26 (5 items) and Frequency block section 27 (5 items).
    4. Say out loud, no notes: your birthday, your phone number, and 3 times of your day (halb neun = 8:30).
    5. Self-score: count correct answers in the drill blocks.

    Self-score rule

    If less than 80% correct, mark the 5 hardest items, wait 24 hours, and re-do exactly those 5.

    • 3 drill blocks done within time.
    • Birthday + phone number + 3 times said aloud.
    • Score >= 80% or re-test list made.

    Tip

    Say numbers and times OUT LOUD. Listening is not reading - your ear must recognize "halb neun = 8:30" automatically.
