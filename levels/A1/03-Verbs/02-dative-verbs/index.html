    German Dative Verbs

    Introduction

    German cases usually work like this:

    • Nominative: the subject - who does the action?
    • Accusative: often the direct object - whom/what?
    • Dative: often the receiver or indirect object - to/for whom?

    For learning, use complete chunks first. Do not calculate every sentence from scratch.

    Dative pronouns

    Meaning Dative pronoun
    to me mir
    to you (informal singular) dir
    to him ihm
    to her ihr
    to it ihm
    to us uns
    to you (informal plural) euch
    to them ihnen
    to you (formal) Ihnen

    Common dative verbs

    1. helfen - to help

    Person Sentence Translation
    me Ich helfe mir. I help myself.
    you Ich helfe dir. I help you.
    him Ich helfe ihm. I help him.
    her Ich helfe ihr. I help her.
    us Ich helfe uns. I help us.
    you all Ich helfe euch. I help you all.
    them Ich helfe ihnen. I help them.
    you formal Ich helfe Ihnen. I help you.

    2. danken - to thank

    Person Sentence Translation
    me Ich danke mir. I thank myself.
    you Ich danke dir. I thank you.
    him Ich danke ihm. I thank him.
    her Ich danke ihr. I thank her.
    us Ich danke uns. I thank us.
    you all Ich danke euch. I thank you all.
    them Ich danke ihnen. I thank them.
    you formal Ich danke Ihnen. I thank you.

    3. gefallen - to appeal to / to be liked by

    Pattern: thing + gefällt/gefallen + dative person.

    Person Sentence Translation
    me Das gefällt mir. I like that.
    you Das gefällt dir. You like that.
    him Das gefällt ihm. He likes that.
    her Das gefällt ihr. She likes that.
    us Das gefällt uns. We like that.
    you all Das gefällt euch. You all like that.
    them Das gefällt ihnen. They like that.
    you formal Das gefällt Ihnen. You like that.

    Plural thing: Die Bücher gefallen mir. = I like the books.

    4. gehören - to belong to

    Person Sentence Translation

    you Das gehört dir. That belongs to you.
    him Das gehört ihm. That belongs to him.
    her Das gehört ihr. That belongs to her.
    us Das gehört uns. That belongs to us.
    you all Das gehört euch. That belongs to you all.
    them Das gehört ihnen. That belongs to them.

    8. fehlen - to be missing / to be missed

    Pattern: person missing + fehlt + dative person.

    Dative person Sentence Translation
    me Ich fehle mir. I am missing to myself.
    you Ich fehle dir. You miss me.
    him Ich fehle ihm. He misses me.
    her Ich fehle ihr. She misses me.
    us Ich fehle uns. We miss me.
    you all Ich fehle euch. You all miss me.
    them Ich fehle ihnen. They miss me.
    you formal Ich fehle Ihnen. You miss me.

    More natural examples: Du fehlst mir. = I miss you. / Er fehlt mir. = I miss him.

    10 chunks to memorize first

    These 10 cover 90% of dative verbs.

    Core chunks to memorize first

    1. Ich helfe ihm/ihr. - I help him/her.
    2. Ich danke ihm/ihr. - I thank him/her.
    3. Das gefällt mir. - I like that.
    4. Das gehört mir. - That belongs to me.
    5. Ich antworte dir. - I answer you.
    6. Ich glaube dir. - I believe you.
    7. Du fehlst mir. - I miss you.
    8. Das schmeckt mir. - I like the taste.
    9. Das passt mir. - That suits me.
    10. Ich höre dir zu. - I listen to you.

    Practice - the dative verb + its person

    Complete each sentence in German with the right pronoun (mir/dir/ihm/ihr/uns/euch/Ihnen) and verb form. Say it out loud, then check. Every verb here forces dative - never use mich/dich after these!

    • Can you help me? (helfen)
    • I thank you. (danken, du)
    • The book belongs to me. (gehören)
    • The film pleases me. (gefallen)
    • That tastes good to me. (schmecken)
    • That suits you. (passen, du)
    • I answer him. (antworten)
    • I congratulate you. (gratulieren, du)
    • You (du) are missing from me. I miss you. (fehlen)
    • I believe you. (glauben, du)
    • I listen to you. (zuhören, du)
    • Does it hurt you? (wehtun, du)
    • I contradict you. (widersprechen, du)
    • That helps us a lot.
    • The shoes fit me well. (passen)
    Antworten - expand to check
    1. Kannst du mir helfen?
    2. Ich danke dir.
    3. Das Buch gehört mir.
    4. Der Film gefällt mir.
    5. Das schmeckt mir.
    6. Das passt dir.
    7. Ich antworte ihm.
    8. Ich gratuliere dir.
    9. Du fehlst mir.
    10. Ich glaube dir.
    11. Ich höre dir zu.
    12. Tut dir das weh?
    13. Ich widerspreche dir.
    14. Das hilft uns sehr.
    15. Die Schuhe passen mir gut.

    Important reminder

    The dative is not always a receiver. With some verbs, German simply requires dative:

    • Ich helfe ihm.
    • Ich danke ihr.
    • Das gefällt mir.

    Learn the verb together with its chunk. The goal is automatic use, not calculating thousands of rules.
