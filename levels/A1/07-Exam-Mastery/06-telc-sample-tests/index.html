    telc A1 (Start Deutsch 1) - Complete Exam Guide & Sample Tests

    Official free resources, exam format, scoring, and strategies. Links verified 2026-08-27.

    1. The exam format (official from telc.net)

    Part Teile Time What happens Points
    Hören (Listening) 3 Teile ca. 20 min 6 short dialogues (a/b/c), 4 announcements (richtig/falsch), 5 phone messages (a/b/c). 15
    Lesen (Reading) 3 Teile 45 min total (shared with Schreiben) 5 short notes/letters (richtig/falsch), 5 ads (a/b), 5 signs (richtig/falsch). 15
    Schreiben (Writing) 2 Teile (same 45 min) (1) Fill a form. (2) Write a short message (official: ca. 30 Wörter). 15
    Sprechen (Speaking) 3 Teile ca. 15 min (1) Introduce yourself. (2) Card game - ask questions. (3) Make requests. 15 (group of 4)

    Total: 60 points. Passing: 36 points (60%). No minimum per section. Each part is scored separately 0-15, and the scores are added.

    3. How each part works (detailed)

    Horen (Listening) - 3 Teile, ca. 20 min

    Teil 1: You hear 6 short conversations. Cross the matching option (a/b/c). You never write - the answer options are printed. Teil 2: You hear 4 short announcements (Durchsagen: station, store, airport). Decide richtig or falsch for each statement. This Teil plays only ONCE. Teil 3: You hear 5 telephone messages. Cross the matching option (a/b/c).

    Strategy (from horen-survival.md): 1. Read the questions BEFORE the audio plays. 2. Listen for KEY words, not every word. Numbers, prices, times, names carry the answer. 3. If you miss something, let it go. The audio does not stop. 4. Numbers 1-20 and the alphabet are your weapon - names and addresses are spelled in Teil 1.

    Lesen (Reading) - 3 Teile, part of the 45 min shared with Schreiben

    Teil 1: Read 5 short notes or letters. Decide richtig or falsch for each statement (5 items). Teil 2: Read 5 ads (Anzeigen). Choose which ad (a or b) fits the situation (5 items). Teil 3: Read 5 signs/notices. Decide richtig or falsch for each statement (5 items).

    Strategy (from lesen-survival.md): 1. Look at the signs FIRST, then read the options. 2. Key vocabulary: geöffnet, geschlossen, verboten, kosten, Bitte. 3. Read one message template from writing-survival.md daily - you train Lesen and Schreiben at the same time.

    Schreiben (Writing) - 2 Teile, part of the 45 min shared with Lesen

    Teil 1: Fill a form with personal data (name, address, birthday, nationality, family status, job). Every empty box loses points.

    Teil 2: Write a short message (official: ca. 30 Wörter - 20-30 words is a safe target): greeting + 2-3 sentences + closing + name. PRESENT TENSE ONLY.

    Your data (memorize for Teil 1):

    • Vorname: Hamed
    • Nachname: Okhovvat
    • Geburtsdatum: 21.09.1985
    • Adresse: Erika-Mann-Straße 12
    • PLZ: 80636
    • Wohnort: München
    • Nationalität: iranisch
    • Familienstand: verheiratet
    • Beruf: Softwareentwickler

    Strategy (from writing-survival.md): 1. Do Teil 1 first - fill every box, it's free points. 2. Teil 2: count your words. 20-30 is the target. 3. Check: present tense? kein/nicht correct? noun caps? 4. Templates cover 95% of tasks: Einladung, Entschuldigung, Dank, Termin, Absage, Information, Bitte.

    Sprechen (Speaking) - 3 Teile, ca. 15 min (group of 4)

    Teil 1: Introduce yourself (2-3 min). Answer questions about your life. Teil 2: Card game (5-6 min). You get a card with a word. Turn it into a W-question for the examiner. Teil 3: Requests and reactions (4 min). You get a picture card. Make a Bitte/Vorschlag/Angebot. React to others.

    Strategy (from speaking-survival.md): 1. Teil 1: Say name, city, job FIRST. The examiner may stop you after 3-4 sentences. 2. Teil 2: Formula = W-word + verb + Sie? ("Wo wohnen Sie?") 3. Teil 3: "Ein Glas Wasser, bitte!" / "Könnte ich bitte... haben?" / "Lass uns...!" 4. Magic reactions: "Ja, gerne!" / "Gleichfalls!" / "Tut mir leid." 5. Emergency: "Einen Moment, bitte." / "Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?"

    4. Scoring breakdown

    Each of the 4 parts is scored 0-15 raw points (60 total). Pass = 36 points (60%), no minimum per section.

    Part Items/points What counts
    Hören Teil 1 6 Correct a/b/c answers
    Hören Teil 2 4 Correct richtig/falsch
    Hören Teil 3 5 Correct a/b/c answers
    Lesen Teil 1 5 Correct richtig/falsch
    Lesen Teil 2 5 Correct a/b answers
    Lesen Teil 3 5 Correct richtig/falsch
    Schreiben Teil 1 ~7 Every filled box
    Schreiben Teil 2 ~8 Content + grammar + length
    Sprechen 15 Teil 1 + Teil 2 + Teil 3 (group of 4)

    36 of 60 = pass. No minimum per section.

    6. Study plan for the last week

    Day Focus Do
    Day 1 Sprechen Teil 1 + 2 Identity script + card questions; do Übungstest Hören once
    Day 2 Sprechen Teil 3 + Schreiben Task 2 Requests + reactions; write one message, get it corrected, rewrite
    Day 3 Schreiben Task 1 + Lesen Fill the form with your data 5 times; do Übungstest Lesen
    Day 4 Hören + numbers Übungstest Hören again; drill numbers, time, spelling
    Day 5 Full mock Lesen + Schreiben (45 min timed) + Sprechen with an agent
    Day 6 Weak areas only Re-test ALL mistakes; memorize corrected sentences
    Day 7 Calm Only cheat sheet + identity + numbers. Sleep well

    7. Exam day checklist

    • ID card (Personalausweis or Reisepass)
    • Confirmation email / Anmeldung
    • Pen (blue or black)
    • Water bottle
    • Arrive 15 min early
    • No phone in the exam room

    8. Common mistakes to avoid

    Mistake Fix
    Empty boxes in Schreiben Teil 1 Fill EVERY box, even if you guess
    Using past tense in Schreiben Present tense ONLY
    Forgetting "Sie" in Sprechen Always "Sie" with examiner, never "du"
    Writing too many words in Task 2 Official: ca. 30 Wörter - keep it 20-35 words
    Panic when you don't understand "Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?"
    Using "Wiedersehen" on phone Say "Auf Wiederhören"
    Forgetting "Gleichfalls!" When examiner wishes you well, say "Gleichfalls!"
