    Frage 277

    Frage: Eine Frau, die ein zweijähriges Kind hat, bewirbt sich in Deutschland um eine Stelle. Was ist ein Beispiel für Diskriminierung? Sie bekommt die Stelle nur deshalb nicht, weil sie …
    English: A woman with a two-year-old child applies for a job in Germany. What is an example of discrimination? She does not get the job for the sole reason that she is …

    # Antwort English
    a kein Englisch spricht. does not speak English.
    b zu hohe Gehaltsvorstellungen hat. has salary expectations that are too high.
    c keine Erfahrungen in diesem Beruf hat. has no experience in this profession.
    d Mutter ist. a mother.
    Lösung / Answer - open after you have chosen

    d) Mutter ist.
    a mother.

    Warum / Why: Germany's General Equal Treatment Act (Allgemeines Gleichbehandlungsgesetz, AGG) prohibits discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, or sexual identity. Refusing a job applicant because she is a mother is gender discrimination - it is illegal regardless of the employer's reasons. The correct answer is that she is a mother (Option D).

    Frage: In den meisten Mietshäusern in Deutschland gibt es eine "Hausordnung". Was steht in einer solchen "Hausordnung"? Sie nennt …
    English: In most rental apartment buildings in Germany there is a "Hausordnung" (house rules). What does such a "Hausordnung" contain? It lists …

    # Antwort English
    a Regeln für die Benutzung öffentlicher Verkehrsmittel. rules for using public transport.

It typically covers quiet hours (Ruhezeiten), use of shared spaces (stairwell, laundry room, courtyard), rubbish disposal, and general conduct. It is usually attached to the rental contract (Mietvertrag). Violations can lead to warnings (Abmahnung) or, in serious cases, termination of the tenancy.

    Frage 280

    Frage: Wenn Sie sich in Deutschland gegen einen falschen Steuerbescheid wehren wollen, müssen Sie …
    English: If you want to contest an incorrect tax assessment notice in Germany, you must …

    # Antwort English
    a nichts machen. do nothing.
    b den Bescheid wegwerfen. throw the notice away.
    c Einspruch einlegen. lodge an objection (Einspruch).
    d warten, bis ein anderer Bescheid kommt. wait for another notice to arrive.
    Lösung / Answer - open after you have chosen

    c) Einspruch einlegen.
    lodge an objection (Einspruch).

    Warum / Why: If you receive a Steuerbescheid (tax assessment notice) that you believe is incorrect, you must formally lodge an Einspruch (objection) with the Finanzamt (tax office) within one month of receiving the notice. If the Einspruch is rejected, you can take the matter to the Finanzgericht (tax court). Simply ignoring or discarding the notice is not a legal remedy and may result in the incorrect amount becoming legally binding.

    Frage 281

    Frage: Zwei Freunde wollen in ein öffentliches Schwimmbad in Deutschland. Beide haben eine dunkle Hautfarbe und werden deshalb nicht hineingelassen. Welches Recht wird in dieser Situation verletzt? Das Recht auf …
    English: Two friends want to enter a public swimming pool in Germany. Both have dark skin and are refused entry for that reason. Which right is being violated in this situation? The right to …

    # Antwort English
    a Meinungsfreiheit freedom of opinion
    b Gleichbehandlung equal treatment
    c Versammlungsfreiheit freedom of assembly
    d Freizügigkeit freedom of movement
    Lösung / Answer - open after you have chosen

    b) Gleichbehandlung

    Warum / Why: Refusing entry to a public facility based on skin colour is racial discrimination, violating the right to Gleichbehandlung (equal treatment) guaranteed by Article 3 of the Basic Law and reinforced by the AGG. Public facilities open to everyone must not discriminate. The other rights listed - freedom of opinion, assembly, and movement - are not relevant to this situation.

    Meinungsfreiheit freedom of speech

    Frage 282

    Frage: Welches Ehrenamt müssen deutsche Staatsbürgerinnen und Staatsbürger übernehmen, wenn sie dazu aufgefordert werden?
    English: Which honorary office must German citizens take on if called upon to do so?

    # Antwort English
    a Vereinstrainerin/Vereinstrainer Club trainer / sports coach
    b Wahlhelferin/Wahlhelfer Election helper / polling station worker
    c Bibliotheksaufsicht Library supervisor
    d Lehrerin/Lehrer Teacher
    Lösung / Answer - open after you have chosen

    b) Wahlhelferin/Wahlhelfer

    Warum / Why: In Germany, citizens can be legally required to serve as Wahlhelfer (polling station workers) during elections. This is one of the few civic duties that can be compelled - similar to jury service in other countries. Wahlhelfer help run the polling station, distribute ballots, and count votes. The duty is part of democratic participation. Other roles like coaching, library work, or teaching cannot be legally compelled.

    deutsch German

The Widerspruch triggers a review process within the authority.

    English: What one needs to know for work is changing very rapidly in the future. What can one do?

Germany has a strong tradition of continuing education (Weiterbildung) and vocational training. Adults are expected to update their skills throughout their careers through further training, courses, and qualification programmes. This concept is reinforced by Germany's dual education system and extensive adult education institutions (Volkshochschulen).

    English: Ms Frost works as a permanent employee in an office. What does she NOT have to pay from her salary?

    # Antwort English
    a Lohnsteuer Income tax (Lohnsteuer)
    b Beiträge zur Arbeitslosenversicherung Unemployment insurance contributions
    c Beiträge zur Renten- und Krankenversicherung Pension and health insurance contributions
    d Umsatzsteuer VAT (Umsatzsteuer / value-added tax)
    Lösung / Answer - open after you have chosen

    d) Umsatzsteuer
    VAT (Umsatzsteuer / value-added tax)

However, she does not pay Umsatzsteuer (VAT / value-added tax) from her salary - VAT is paid by businesses on goods and services, not by employees from their wages.

    Frage 286

    Frage: Welche Organisation in einer Firma hilft den Arbeitnehmerinnen und Arbeitnehmern bei Problemen mit der Arbeitgeberin/dem Arbeitgeber?
    English: Which organisation in a company helps employees with problems involving the employer?

    # Antwort English
    a der Betriebsrat the works council (Betriebsrat)
    b die Betriebsprüferin/der Betriebsprüfer the company auditor
    c die Betriebsgruppe the company group
    d das Betriebsmanagement the company management
    Lösung / Answer - open after you have chosen

    a) der Betriebsrat
    the works council (Betriebsrat)

    Warum / Why: The Betriebsrat (works council) is an elected body that represents the interests of employees in companies with five or more staff. It has co-determination rights (Mitbestimmungsrechte) on matters such as working hours, holiday schedules, redundancies, and workplace safety. Employees can approach the Betriebsrat with disputes or grievances about their employer. The Betriebsrat is legally protected and independent of management.

    helfen to help

    Lösung / Answer - open after you have chosen

    b) den Fernseher reklamieren
    make a complaint / return the television (reklamieren)

    Warum / Why: In Germany, if you buy a defective product, you have the right to Gewährleistung (statutory warranty) for two years from the date of purchase.
