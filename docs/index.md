# Deutsch Book

Your complete German course from zero to A2 - grammar, verbs, sentence building, vocabulary, daily communication, exam mastery, and practice. Built from your own knowledge base, organized like a real book.

## Welcome

This book takes you from zero to A2 solid - A1 fundamentals first, then A2 Aufbau, then everyday speaking. It is not a random collection of notes: it is parts that build on each other, exactly like a course.

## How to Use This Book

### For Best Results

1. **Start at Part 01** - read the Part index first, then the files in numbered order
2. **Follow the sequence** - each part builds on the previous one
3. **Say things out loud** - listening and speaking beat reading alone
4. **Learn nouns with articles** - always der/die/das, never a bare noun
5. **Use Part 08 daily** - drill sentences, log mistakes, re-test them
6. **Review regularly** - revisit parts to reinforce what you learned

### Study Tips

- **Consistency over intensity** - 30 minutes daily beats 3 hours weekly
- **Active practice** - cover the German, recall English → German, say it out loud
- **Track your progress** - strike through mistakes only after you get them right from memory
- **Speak early, speak often** - imperfect German that flows beats perfect German that stops
