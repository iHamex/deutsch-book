# Mistake Log - Leben in Deutschland

> Every wrong answer goes here until you can produce the correct fact from memory three times. This is the difference between "I have read the book" and "I am ready".

[Overview](index.md) | [Mock test](mock-test.md)

---

## 1. How to log a mistake

1. Write the question number and the topic in one line.
2. Write what you chose and why you chose it.
3. Write the correct answer as a full German sentence.
4. Write the one word or rule that decides the answer.
5. Re-test it after two days, then after one week.

## 3. Weak spots

- [ ] Dates and history (1945, 1949, 1961, 1989, 1990)
- [ ] Federal organs and who elects whom
- [ ] State facts: capital, flag, Landtag, leader title
- [ ] Numbers: 16 states, 33 questions, 17 to pass, 5% threshold
- [ ] Europe: EU members, Schengen, euro
- [ ] Everyday law: Jugendamt, Kündigung, Mutterschutz, Miete

---

## 4. When a mistake is closed

!!! success "Close an entry only when"
    You have answered the same fact correctly three times, on three different days, without looking. Then strike the entry with `~~text~~` and move it to the bottom of the table. Do not delete it - the history shows what you have mastered.

---

## 5. Clean review

- [ ] Every open entry has a full German sentence in the "Correct answer" column.
- [ ] Every open entry has a re-test date.
- [ ] No entry is older than one week without a re-test.
- [ ] The weak-spot list matches the current open entries.
