## 2. What the state questions always ask

| Question | What to answer with |
|---|---|
| Coat of arms | The picture with the state colours and the state animal |
| District / city district | A name that belongs to that state only |
| Term of parliament | 5 years everywhere except Bremen (4 years) |
| Voting age (local) | 16 in most states, 18 in Bavaria, Hesse, Rhineland-Palatinate, Saarland and Saxony |
| Flag colours | Say them in German, in the official order |
| Political information | The Landeszentrale für politische Bildung |
| Capital | The city in the table above, not the largest city |
| State shape on the map | Compare the outline, not the size |
| Head of government | Ministerpräsident(in) everywhere except Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg |
| Missing ministry | The state has no foreign ministry and no defence ministry |

!!! tip "Three facts cover most state questions"
    **Capital, flag, head of government.** If you know those three for your Bundesland, eight of the ten questions are already answerable.

!!! warning "Berlin, Bremen and Hamburg are the exceptions"
    They are city-states: no Landtag, no Ministerpräsident. Berlin has a Regierender Bürgermeister and an Abgeordnetenhaus, Bremen and Hamburg have a Bürgerschaft and a First Mayor or Senate President.

## 3. Practice order

1. Learn your own state page completely.
2. Read this table once, cover the capital column, and say each capital aloud.
3. Cover the flag column and produce the colours in German.
4. Open three random state pages and score yourself out of 30.
