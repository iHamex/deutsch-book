# Leben in Deutschland - Complete Question Bank

> All 460 official questions for the Leben in Deutschland test and the Einbürgerungstest, rebuilt as a clean study book. Every question has its own section, an English translation of the question and all four options, the correct answer behind a collapsible panel, a short explanation, and a vocabulary table.

---

## 1. What this section contains

- **300 general questions** in ten sets of 30.
- **160 state questions** in 16 pages of 10, one page per Bundesland.
- **Bavaria is your exam set** because you live in Munich; the other 15 pages are there so you can recognise every state fact.
- **Every answer is hidden** until you expand it, so each page works as a mock test.
- **One vocabulary table per question** with the German words in dictionary form and their English meaning - about 3,300 entries in total.
- **[States at a glance](states-at-a-glance.md)** puts capital, flag, parliament, term and voting age of all 16 Bundesländer in one table.

!!! info "Source and accuracy"
    The German questions and options follow the official BAMF *Gesamtfragenkatalog zum Test Leben in Deutschland* (Stand 07.05.2025). The official figures (coat of arms, flags, ballot papers, maps) are extracted from the same PDF. English translations, explanations and vocabulary are study aids - the real exam is in German only.

## 2. The exam in one table

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Questions | 33 |
| Time | 60 minutes |
| Composition | 30 general questions + 3 questions from your Bundesland |
| Pass mark | 17 correct answers |
| Language | German |
| Question pool | 300 general + 10 per state (460 total) |

!!! tip "The 3-second rule"
    If you cannot choose an answer within 3 seconds, mark the question and move on. Come back at the end. The test rewards calm recognition, not long deliberation.

## 3. How to use this section

1. Read the German question and cover the options with your hand after reading them once.
2. Say your answer aloud, then write the letter on paper. Do not click or expand yet.
3. Expand **Lösung / Answer** and check only the letter first.
4. Read the explanation and say the correct sentence aloud once.
5. Read the vocabulary table and translate each word back into German.
6. Log every wrong answer in the [mistake log](mistakes.md), then re-test it after two days.

!!! tip "How to use the vocabulary table"
    Each question has its own table with the German words from the question and the correct answer in their dictionary form: nouns with `the`, verbs with `to`. Cover the English column and say the meaning, then cover the German column and produce the word. A word you cannot produce in both directions is not learned yet.

!!! info "Two kinds of translation"
    The block under the question translates the **whole sentence**, so you always know exactly what is being asked. The vocabulary table teaches the **individual words** you need to read the next question without help. Words are listed in the order they appear in the question, then the correct answer, then the other options.

!!! warning "Do not learn the letter"
    The real test draws questions at random from the 460-question pool, and the options keep their official order. Learn the **content and the key words**, not "question 57 is b". If you meet the same fact in a different wording, you must still recognise it.

## 9. A clean finish

!!! quote "One page, one habit"
    Do not read this book. Work one page, answer on paper, open the solutions, write down the misses. A page you have answered is worth ten pages you have only read.

!!! success "You are ready when"
    You can answer 30 random general questions and 3 random Bavaria questions in 60 minutes with at least 17 correct - twice in a row, on different days, without notes. That is the same standard as the real test.
