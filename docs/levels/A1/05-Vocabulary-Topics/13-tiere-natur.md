## Practice - Say it from memory

Translate each sentence. Say it out loud, then check.

- I have a dog and a cat.
- The dog barks. The cat meows.
- The bird is singing in the tree.
- I live near a forest.
- The river is very long.
- The mountain is very high.
- I love nature.
- The flowers are beautiful.
- The weather is nice. The sun is shining.
- We have to protect the environment.
