## How this Part connects

- Before: [04-Sentence-Building](../04-Sentence-Building/00-INDEX.md) (you can build sentences, now fill them with words)
- After: [06-Daily-Communication](../06-Daily-Communication/00-INDEX.md) (turn words into natural phrases)
- Practice: after each file, write 3 sentences about your own life (Meine Wohnung hat... / Ich brauche...).

## What you can do after this Part

- Introduce yourself and greet people in German.
- Describe your family, apartment, and health.
- Talk about food, shopping, transport, weather, and feelings.
- Ask for and give directions.
- Use opposites to describe quickly.

## Tip

Learn nouns WITH article: not "Tisch = table" but "der Tisch". Every file includes the correct articles for nouns.
