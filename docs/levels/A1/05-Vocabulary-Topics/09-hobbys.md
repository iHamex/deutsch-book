## 9. Talking about hobbies - Ueber Hobbys sprechen

| Ich lese gern. | I like to read. |

| Ich interessiere mich für Fotografie. | I'm interested in photography. |

## Practice - Say it from memory

Translate each sentence. Say it out loud, then check.

- What are your hobbies? - I like to read and swim.
- I'm interested in photography.
- What do you do in your free time? - I go to the cinema and meet friends.
- How often do you go swimming? - Twice a week.
- Do you feel like going to the cinema? - Yes, great idea!
- What are you doing this weekend? - I'm going hiking.
- I always read in the evening.
- I sometimes cook.
- I never play video games.
- My favourite hobby is playing guitar.
