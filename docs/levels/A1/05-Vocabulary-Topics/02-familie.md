# Family - Familie

> Talking about your family is one of the first things you do in German - in exam introductions, at work, or with friends. Learn the words, then build sentences about YOUR family.

## 5. Possessive articles with family - Possessivartikel mit Familie

| German | English | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| meine Mutter | my mother | Das ist meine Mutter. (This is my mother.) |
| mein Vater | my father | Das ist mein Vater. (This is my father.) |
| meine Eltern | my parents | Das sind meine Eltern. (These are my parents.) |
| mein Bruder | my brother | Das ist mein Bruder. (This is my brother.) |
| meine Schwester | my sister | Das ist meine Schwester. (This is my sister.) |
| mein Mann | my husband | Das ist mein Mann. (This is my husband.) |
| meine Frau | my wife | Das ist meine Frau. (This is my wife.) |
| mein Kind | my child | Das ist mein Kind. (This is my child.) |

!!! tip "Family articles"
    - **die** Mutter, **die** Schwester, **die** Frau, **die** Tochter, **die** Eltern, **die** Kinder = feminine or plural
    - **der** Vater, **der** Bruder, **der** Mann, **der** Sohn = masculine
    - **das** Kind = neuter
