# Greetings and Introductions - Gruessen und Vorstellen

> Your first words in German. Master these and you can walk into any room, answer any phone call, and introduce yourself without panic. Every sentence here is something you will actually say today.

---

## 1. Greetings - Gruessungen

| German | When to use | English |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Guten Morgen! | morning until ~10 | Good morning! |
| Guten Tag! | daytime, formal | Good day! |
| Guten Abend! | evening | Good evening! |
| Hallo! | informal, any time | Hi! |
| Hey! | very informal, friends | Hey! |
| Moin! | northern Germany, informal | Hi! / Morning! |
| Servus! | Bavaria/Austria, informal | Hi! / Bye! |
| Grüß Gott! | Bavaria/Austria, formal | Hello! (literally: greet God) |

## 11. Formal vs informal - Sie vs du

!!! info "When to use Sie vs du"
    - **Sie** = formal, with strangers, at work, with older people, at the doctor, at the Amt
    - **du** = informal, with friends, family, children, people your age in casual settings
    - When in doubt, use **Sie** - it's always safer

| Situation | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| At the doctor | Sie |
| At the Amt (government office) | Sie |
| With your boss | Sie |
| With colleagues at work | Sie (until they say "du") |
| With friends | du |
| With family | du |
| With children | du |
| With neighbours (first meeting) | Sie |
| With neighbours (after years) | du |
