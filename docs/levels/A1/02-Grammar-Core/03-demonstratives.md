# Demonstratives - dieser / diese / dieses (this) - A1

> Point at something near you. English "this / that" = German "dieser / diese / dieses". A1 needs this for shopping, directions, and everyday pointing.

---

## 1. The one rule

**dieser copies the article of the noun, in the correct case.** It declines exactly like **der/die/das**.

You already know der -> den -> dem. Do the same with dieser.

**Meaning:**

- dieser / diese / dieses = this
- diese (plural) = these
- In spoken German, dieser often also means "that" (distance is shown by pointing, not by a different word). For "that one over there", Germans add **da/dort**: dieser da.

---

## 2. Full table (all 3 cases you need)

| | Nominativ | Akkusativ | Dativ |
|---|---|---|---|
| m | **dieser** Mann | **diesen** Mann | **diesem** Mann |
| f | **diese** Frau | **diese** Frau | **dieser** Frau |
| n | **dieses** Kind | **dieses** Kind | **diesem** Kind |
| pl | **diese** Kinder | **diese** Kinder | **diesen** Kindern |

!!! tip "Same as der"
    dieser -> diesen -> diesem mirrors der -> den -> dem. Learn one, you know all.

> **Pattern:** masculine changes twice (dieser -> diesen -> diesem), feminine changes only in dative (diese -> dieser), neuter changes only in dative (dieses -> diesem), plural dative adds -n.

Compare to your known tables:

| article | m nom | m akk | m dat |
|---|---|---|---|
| der | der Mann | den Mann | dem Mann |
| dieser | dieser Mann | diesen Mann | diesem Mann |
| ein | ein Mann | einen Mann | einem Mann |

**Same endings:** -er, -en, -em for masculine. Learn one, you know all three.

---

## 3. With adjectives

When an adjective comes between dieser and the noun, the adjective is always **-e or -en** (same as after der):

| | example | why |
|---|---|---|
| m nom | **dieser alte** Mann | dieser does the work, adjective just -e |
| m akk | **diesen alten** Mann | -en after den/diesen |
| m dat | **diesem alten** Mann | -en after dem/diesem |
| f | **diese neue** Wohnung | -e |
| n | **dieses kleine** Kind | -e |
| pl | **diese neuen** Schuhe | -en (plural always -en) |

- Dieser teure Mantel gefällt mir nicht. - I do not like this expensive coat.
- Ich nehme diesen Pullover. - I will take this sweater.
- Mit diesem alten Auto fahre ich nicht. - I do not drive with this old car.

---

## 4. dieser vs der as pointing words

Both can point, but use them differently:

| word | use | example |
|---|---|---|
| **dieser** | point to one thing clearly - shopping, showing | **Dieser** Kaffee ist zu heiß. (this coffee here) |
| **der** (as demonstrative) | point back to something already mentioned - spoken | **Der** da! (that guy over there!) |
| **das** | neutral "that/this" for a whole idea | **Das** ist gut. / **Das** stimmt. |

At A1, use **dieser** when you want to say "this/these".

---

## 5. Real sentences - shopping, at home, on the street

**Shopping (most important for dieser):**

- Wie viel kostet **dieser** Pullover? - How much is this sweater?
- **Diese** Hose passt mir nicht. - These trousers do not fit me.
- Ich möchte **dieses** Hemd probieren. - I would like to try on this shirt.
- Haben Sie **diese** Schuhe in Größe 43? - Do you have these shoes in size 43?
- **Dieser** Mantel ist zu teuer. - This coat is too expensive.

**At home / with people:**

- **Dieser** Mann ist mein Chef. - This man is my boss.
- **Diese** Frau wohnt nebenan. - This woman lives next door.
- **Dieses** Kind spielt im Garten. - This child plays in the garden.
- Kennst du **diesen** Film? - Do you know this film?

**Directions:**

- **Diese** Straße führt zum Bahnhof. - This street leads to the station.
- In **diesem** Haus wohne ich. - I live in this building.
- An **dieser** Haltestelle steige ich aus. - I get off at this stop.
