> Read this once. It explains how the new phase works and how to start a session.  
> The other files (AGENTS.md, RULES.md, MEMORY.md) are for the agents. This file is for you.

## 1. What changed

You finished the A1 exam phase. Now the goal is NOT an exam. The goal is: speak German
naturally in everyday life - at Check24, in shops, on the S-Bahn, at the doctor, at the
Rathaus, with neighbors, with friends.

Move from "I know the words" to "I say the words without thinking."

## 3. How to start a session (short commands - nothing to copy)

The root AGENTS.md knows these triggers. All of them make the agent read this phase's files
(AGENTS.md, RULES.md, MEMORY.md) itself and resume from the session log:

> Let's practice speaking.  
> Go to the speaking directory and continue where we left off.  
> Sprachphase starten - wo haben wir aufgehört?

That is it. The agent starts a German conversation immediately - no explanation, no exam,
no quiz. You talk, the agent corrects lightly and keeps the conversation alive.

To pick a topic yourself, add it:

> ... Let's practice speaking. Thema: [im Supermarkt / beim Arzt / Smalltalk mit Kollegen / standup / ...]

Or let the agent pick from [situations.md](situations.md) - real situations: work, shops, DHL,
Bahn, Bürgerbüro, Ausländerbehörde, bank, telephone, apartment viewing, new people,
technical talk, job interview, casual chats.

## 4. The 10 rules of this phase (in short)

1. You SPEAK. The agent asks one German question, you answer, it reacts. No long lectures.
2. German first. The agent will not translate everything - simple German first, English or
   Persian only when you are truly stuck.
3. Corrections are light. Important mistakes get corrected, tiny ones pass. The flow survives.
4. Forgotten words get RECYCLED - the agent sneaks them back into later conversations.
5. No instant answers. The agent hints first, gives you a chance to remember.
6. No stop for a forgotten word. You learn to describe: "Das ist das Ding, wo man ...".
7. Real situations, not textbook scenes. Munich, Check24, your real errands.
8. Grammar through speaking. Repeated mistake -> short rule + 2-3 examples, keep going
   (watchlist: word order, Akkusativ/Dativ, der/die/das, prepositions, Perfekt, weil/dass/wenn).
9. Difficulty goes UP toward B1 - no artificial A1 ceiling.
10. Comfort over perfection. Imperfect Deutsch that flows beats perfect Deutsch that stops.

## 5. The end-of-session review (you get this every longer session)

- Today's useful words: 3-7
- Today's mistakes: 2-5 (wrong -> right)
- Better sentences: 2-5 to remember
- One thing to practice next time

Short. Then the agent updates [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md) so nothing is lost.

## 6. Small habits that double your progress

- Before each session, say out loud 2 spoken-German rules from [01-daily-conversations.md](../06-Daily-Communication/01-daily-conversations.md)
  (the spoken cheat sheet: ich hab, mal, doch, V1 statements). The cheat sheet is the fast
  path to sounding natural.
- After each session, use ONE corrected sentence 3 times during your real day
  (with a colleague, in a shop, in your head at the S-Bahn).
- Tell the agent about your real week: the real DHL trip, the real standup. It becomes the
  next session's situation - so the next real event is never your first time saying it.
