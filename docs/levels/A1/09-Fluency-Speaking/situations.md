# Situations - Real-Life German Practice

> The situation bank for the ../09-Fluency-Speaking/ phase. Pick ONE situation per session, make it
> realistic, role-play both sides, change the variables next time. A situation is a
> conversation, not a vocabulary list. Use Hamed's real identity (MEMORY.md section 1) and
> his real city (Munich) whenever useful.

---

## How to run a situation

1. The coach sets the scene in ONE German sentence: "Du bist im Supermarkt. Du suchst
   Tomaten." (You are in the supermarket. You are looking for tomatoes.)
2. The coach plays the other person (cashier, colleague, doctor, officer ...).
3. Hamed reacts, asks, answers. The coach reacts naturally back.
4. Small correction loop inside the flow (see AGENTS.md section 2) - never stop the scene
   for a lecture.
5. When the scene ends naturally, debrief in 2-3 lines max: one good thing + one thing to
   fix + the natural sentence for what he struggled with.
6. Log new forgotten words in MEMORY.md recycle log.

Change at least ONE variable each time the same situation reappears:
different time, different problem, different route, harder follow-up, past tense version
("Gestern warst du beim Arzt. Was hast du erzählt?").

## 1. Work - colleagues

- Small talk in the kitchen: weekend, weather, lunch plans.
- Meeting: say what you did, ask what the next step is, agree on a time.
- Ask a colleague for help with a task.
- Tell your team you are learning German - they should switch to German with you.
- Lunch: decide together where to eat (ich hab Lust auf ..., lass uns ...).
- Technical topic: explain a bug or a feature in simple German (das Programm macht ...,
  es funktioniert nicht, wir müssen das ändern).
- Feedback: say what you like and what you would change, politely.

## 2. Introducing yourself (revisit, now for real life)

- Party or new team: name, job, where you live, where you are from, hobbies.
- Answer follow-ups: How do you find Munich? How long have you been here? Family?
- Introduce yourself to a neighbor (first meeting at the door).

## 3. Weekend talk (small talk classic)

- What did you do on the weekend? (past tense!)
- What are you doing next weekend? (plans, lass uns ...)
- React to the other person's weekend (Echt? Das klingt gut. Na dann los.).

## 4. Shopping

- Supermarket: find a product, ask where something is, pay, bag your things.
- Bakery / butcher: order by pointing + numbers, ask for a smaller piece.
- Clothing store: ask for a size, try on, complain it is too small, pay.
- Return something: "Ich möchte das zurückgeben." (defective, wrong size).
- Post office / DHL: send a package, choose the cheap option, fill the form.

## 5. Restaurant and café

- Get a table, order drinks, order food, ask what something is, say it tastes good,
  ask for the bill, pay, split the bill.
- Complain: the food is cold, the order is wrong, we are waiting too long.
- With friends: decide what to eat, comment on the food, propose dessert.

## 6. Train station and public transport (MVV in Munich)

- Buy a ticket at the machine or counter: type, zone, price.
- Ask which platform (Gleis), when the train leaves, if it is late.
- Validate your ticket, find your platform, handle a delay announcement.
- Ask a stranger if this is the right direction (Fährt dieser Zug nach ...?).
- Lost: ask for the way to the bus stop, to Marienplatz, to your street.

## 7. Doctor (Hausarzt, Apotheke)

- Make an appointment by phone: name, reason, time.
- At reception: say your name, show your insurance card (Versichertenkarte).
- Tell the doctor what hurts: since when, how strong, what makes it better/worse.
- Pharmacy: ask for something for headache/cold, explain symptoms, pay.

## 8. Bürgerbüro / Rathaus

- Book an appointment (online or phone), state the reason.
- Anmeldung: give your data, show passport + landlord confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung).
- Ask what documents you need (Welche Unterlagen brauche ich?).
- Find out opening hours, wait, confirm your appointment time.

## 9. Ausländerbehörde

- Ask when your appointment is, what papers you need (Pass, Foto, Arbeitgeber, Miete).
- Explain a problem: my appointment is too late, I need the document urgently,
  my address has changed.
- Confirm in your own words what they told you (Ich verstehe: ich brauche ...).

## 10. Bank

- Open an account: name, address, passport, phone number.
- Ask for a card, set the PIN, ask about fees.
- Transfer money: ask for the IBAN, confirm the amount, ask when it arrives.
- Problem: card blocked, money missing, app not working.

## 11. DHL / Post

- Ask how much a package costs, choose shipping speed, fill the customs form.
- Package was not delivered: ask where to pick it up (Filiale / Packstation e.g. Kiosk).
- Return a package you ordered, get the return label, drop it off.
- Buy stamps, send a letter, ask how long it takes.

## 12. Phone calls

- Answer the phone: "Hallo, hier ist Hamed." Ask who it is.
- Make a call: state your name, who you want, the reason briefly.
- Bad connection: ask to repeat, speak slower, call back.
- Office calls: leave a message, take a message, confirm the next step.
- Machine (Anrufbeantworter): understand the message, leave a short message.

## 13. Appointments

- By phone: propose a time, refuse politely, suggest another time.
- Confirm by email/text: "Ich bestätige den Termin am ... um ...".
- Cancel or reschedule: say sorry, give the reason, propose a new time.
- Arrive, check in, wait, ask how long it will take.

## 14. Renting an apartment (classic for Munich)

- Viewing: greet the landlord, ask about rent, rooms, heating, move-in date.
- Say what you are looking for: 1-2 rooms, quiet, near the U-Bahn, up to X Euro.
- Documents: tell them what you have (Verdienstbescheinigung, Schufa, Mietschuldenfreiheit).
- Ask about the contract details: deposit (Kaution), running costs (Nebenkosten).

## 15. Neighbors

- First meeting: name, where you are from, niceties, offer help (Wenn Sie Hilfe brauchen...).
- Greeting in the stairwell: "Guten Morgen!", comment on the weather.
- Complaint (gently): noise, packages for each other, parking.
- Ask for a favor: water for the plants, a tool, hold the door.

## 16. Meeting new people (social)

- At a party: introduce yourself, ask what they do, find common ground.
- Exchange Numbers or WhatsApp, plan to meet.
- Say goodbye properly: "War schön, dich kennenzulernen. Bis bald!"

## 17. Job interview (German practice, not exam)

- "Erzählen Sie mir etwas über sich." - your path, your job at Check24, your stack.
- Why this job? What is important to you in a team?
- Salary and contract: ask about the range, holidays, remote.
- Questions back: team size, product, technologies ("Was benutzt ihr für ...?").
- Talking about experience: what you built, what you are good at (Ich arbeite mit ...,
  Ich bin gut im ...).

## 18. Technical / software topics (his daily reality)

- Explain what you are working on right now, simply.
- Ask technical questions in German: "Wie machst du das?", "Warum ist das so?"
- Pair programming: suggest, explain, ask for help (Kannst du mal schauen?).
- Standup: past (gestern), present (heute), future (morgen).

## 19. Casual conversations (no goal, just chat)

- Complain together about the weather / the S-Bahn / Munich traffic.
- Recommend a film, series, or restaurant; react to one.
- Discuss: are you a morning person? Do you cook? Where would you travel?
- Small opinions: "Was denkst du über ...?" - real opinions, one or two sentences.
- Tell a short funny story from your week (feel free to use English words you know).

## Extend this bank

The coach may add situations from Hamed's real life: tell us what actually happened this
week (the real DHL run, the real Rathaus visit, the real standup) and it becomes tomorrow's
situation. The goal: the next real event is never the first time he says those words.
