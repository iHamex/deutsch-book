## 2. Current level snapshot (2026-09-02)

Strong areas:
- Identity talk, numbers, time, dates, greetings, polite requests - solid.
- Spoken cheat sheet habits: drop the -e, mal, doch, fillers - knows them, needs to USE
  them automatically. These are the fastest win for naturalness.
- Grammar foundations: V2 word order, weil/dass/wenn verb-final, kein/nicht,
  N/A/D articles, separables, modals, Perfekt basics.

Focus areas (coach, bring these back in EVERY session):
- der/die/das gender recall (especially for everyday objects).
- Akkusativ vs Dativ after verbs and prepositions (mich/mir, den/dem).
- Adjective endings at natural chunk level (ein neues Handy, die neue Wohnung).
- Verb at position 2 under real conversation pressure (Gestern BIN ich ...).
- Präteritum of common verbs: war, hatte, ging, kam, wollte, konnte - recognition solid,
  active use needs practice.
- Perfekt with sein (kommen, gehen, fahren, etc.) - not just haben.
- nach Hause (direction) vs zu Hause (location).
- zu + Infinitiv structure (keine Chance, Lust, Zeit + zu + verb).
- seit for duration (not für) - takes dative.
- Stop translating in his head: react fast, accept imperfect, keep the pace up.
