## How to start a session

Say: `Let's practice speaking` or `weiter` or `continue where we left off`

The agent reads AGENTS.md + RULES.md + MEMORY.md and starts a German conversation immediately - one question at a time, corrects lightly, keeps it alive.

To pick a topic: `Let's practice speaking. Thema: im Supermarkt`

---

## How this connects

- Before: all Parts 01-06 (you need words and grammar to speak)
- After speaking: agent updates MEMORY.md recycle log + mistakes -> those words come back naturally next session.
- Use [06-Daily-Communication](../06-Daily-Communication/00-INDEX.md) phrases in speaking - they are the spoken style this Part trains.

## Tip

Comfort over perfection. Imperfect German that flows beats perfect German that stops. Use fillers from 06 (mal, doch, naja) here - they are welcome, not penalized.
