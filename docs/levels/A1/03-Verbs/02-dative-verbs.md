# German Dative Verbs

## Introduction

German cases usually work like this:

- **Nominative:** the subject - who does the action?
- **Accusative:** often the direct object - whom/what?
- **Dative:** often the receiver or indirect object - to/for whom?

For learning, use complete chunks first. Do not calculate every sentence from scratch.

## Dative pronouns

| Meaning | Dative pronoun |
|---|---|
| to me | **mir** |
| to you (informal singular) | **dir** |
| to him | **ihm** |
| to her | **ihr** |
| to it | **ihm** |
| to us | **uns** |
| to you (informal plural) | **euch** |
| to them | **ihnen** |
| to you (formal) | **Ihnen** |

## Core chunks to memorize first

1. **Ich helfe ihm/ihr.** - I help him/her.
2. **Ich danke ihm/ihr.** - I thank him/her.
3. **Das gefällt mir.** - I like that.
4. **Das gehört mir.** - That belongs to me.
5. **Ich antworte dir.** - I answer you.
6. **Ich glaube dir.** - I believe you.
7. **Du fehlst mir.** - I miss you.
8. **Das schmeckt mir.** - I like the taste.
9. **Das passt mir.** - That suits me.
10. **Ich höre dir zu.** - I listen to you.

## Practice - the dative verb + its person

Complete each sentence in German with the right pronoun (mir/dir/ihm/ihr/uns/euch/Ihnen) and verb form. Say it out loud, then check. Every verb here forces dative - never use mich/dich after these!

- Can you help me? (helfen)
- I thank you. (danken, du)
- The book belongs to me. (gehören)
- The film pleases me. (gefallen)
- That tastes good to me. (schmecken)
- That suits you. (passen, du)
- I answer him. (antworten)
- I congratulate you. (gratulieren, du)
- You (du) are missing from me. I miss you. (fehlen)
- I believe you. (glauben, du)
- I listen to you. (zuhören, du)
- Does it hurt you? (wehtun, du)
- I contradict you. (widersprechen, du)
- That helps us a lot.
- The shoes fit me well. (passen)

## Important reminder

The dative is not always a receiver. With some verbs, German simply requires dative:

- **Ich helfe ihm.**
- **Ich danke ihr.**
- **Das gefällt mir.**

Learn the verb together with its chunk. The goal is automatic use, not calculating thousands of rules.
