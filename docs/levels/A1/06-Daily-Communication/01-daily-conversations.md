# Daily Conversations - Alltagskonversationen

> **Why this file exists:** Textbooks teach you "How are you?" and "I would like a coffee." But in real life you say "To be honest..." and "Just kidding!" and "How do you even say this in German?" This file fills that gap - and it teaches you the **spoken** version, the way Germans actually talk day to day, not the textbook version.
>
> **The golden rule:** in every row below, the phrase on the left is what you SAY. The (full form) in parentheses is the correct textbook version - know both, but say the spoken one.

---

## ⚡ Spoken German Cheat Sheet (read this first)

German spoken daily is shorter than written German. Five changes make you sound natural instantly.

**The 5 changes:**

1. **Drop the -e on verbs** - ich habe → **ich hab**, ich mache → **ich mach**, ich glaube → **ich glaub**. Nobody says "ich habe" in daily talk.

2. **mal = the magic word** - *Kannst du mir **mal** helfen?* - "real quick / just". It softens every request.

3. **Shorten small words** - etwas → **was**, ein bisschen → **bisschen**, einmal → **mal**, eine → **'ne** (Ich schick dir **'ne** Mail).

4. **Drop obvious words** - **Stimmt.** / **Weiß nicht.** / **Keine Ahnung.** / **Tut mir leid.** - *das*, *ich*, *es* disappear when clear.

5. **Add fillers** - **na ja, halt, eben, so, irgendwie, weißt du, oder?** - They make you sound human, not a robot.

!!! tip "Bonus everyday words"
    - **Prost!** (cheers)
    - **zahlen** (the bill) - just say "Zahlen, bitte!"
    - **die Karte** (the menu) - "Kann ich die Karte haben?"
    - **kaputt** (exhausted) - very common for müde
    - **sauer** (angry) - "Ich bin sauer" is the daily way
    - **klar** (sure) - "Na klar!" = of course

### Rule 2: mal = the magic word - softens every request

| Textbook (without mal) | Spoken (with mal) | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Kannst du mir helfen? | **Kannst du mir mal helfen?** | friendly, not bossy |
| Ruf mich an! | **Ruf mich mal an!** | casual, warm |
| Schreib mir! | **Schreib mir mal!** | casual |
| Zeig mir das! | **Zeig mal!** | relaxed |
| Warte! | **Warte mal!** | soft |
| Komm her! | **Komm mal her!** | friendly |
| Moment! | **Moment mal!** | hold on a sec |
| Wie sagt man...? | **Wie sagt man noch mal...?** | thinking out loud |


---

### Rule 2b: Lass + mal = the daily combo (sounds instantly natural)

| Spoken | Meaning | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Lass mal!** | Forget it! / Leave it! / Never mind! | Someone is doing or suggesting something - you stop them |
| **Lass mal, ich mach das.** | Never mind, I'll do it. | You take over instead of waiting for them |
| **Lass mich mal machen!** | Let me do it! | You want to try it yourself right now |
| **Lass uns mal gehen.** | Let's just go. | Gentle push to leave |
| **Lass mich mal sehen.** | Let me have a look. | You want to check something first |
| **Lass mich dir das zeigen.** | Let me show you that. | You show something on your screen or on paper |
| **Lass mich dir das erklären.** | Let me explain it to you. | You take over the explaining |
| **Lass mich dir helfen.** | Let me help you. | A colleague is stuck on something |
| **Lass mich dir eine Frage stellen.** | Let me ask you a question. | Polite way to start asking |
| **Lass mich kurz überlegen.** | Let me think for a second. | Buying time - softer than "Moment mal!" |
| **Lass mich das machen.** | Let me do that. | You take the task over |
| **Lass mich das noch mal lesen.** | Let me read that again. | You want to check something first |
| **Lass mich dir eine Mail schicken.** | Let me send you an email. | Work classic - follow-up after a talk |

> **The pattern:** Lass mich + dir/das + verb - "let me + ..." Add **mal** to soften it:
> *Lass mich dir das **mal** zeigen!* (Let me just show you!) / *Lass mich dir das **mal** erklären.*
>
> **The work classic:** *"Soll ich das machen?" → "Lass mal, ich mach das!"* - instantly natural, zero textbook.
>
> **Formal version (customers, strangers):** *Lassen Sie mich das zeigen.* / *Lassen Sie mich kurz überlegen.* - same idea, Sie form.

### Rule 5: Add fillers (sound human, not robotic)

| Filler | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| na ja | well / eh | **Na ja**, vielleicht. (Well, maybe.) |
| halt | just (that's how it is) | Ich hab **halt** keine Zeit. (I just don't have time.) |
| eben | exactly / just | OK, dann sind wir **eben** verschiedener Meinung. (Then we just disagree.) |
| so | like / about | **So**, was machen wir? (So, what are we doing?) |
| irgendwie | somehow | Das ist **irgendwie** komisch. (That's somehow weird.) |
| weißt du | you know | **Weißt du**, das ist gut. (You know, that's good.) |
| oder? | right? / isn't it? | Das ist gut, **oder**? (That's good, right?) |
| mal schauen | we'll see | **Mal schauen**, was passiert. (We'll see what happens.) |

### Rule 6: Pronoun shrinking (in fast, casual speech pronouns melt into the verb)

| Full form | Spoken | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hast du | **haste** | Haste mal 'ne Sekunde? (Got a second?) |
| bist du | **biste** | Biste sicher? (Are you sure?) |
| habe ich | **hab ich** → **hab'sch** | Hab'sch vergessen. (I forgot.) |
| ist es | **is' es** → **ists** | Wie is'es? (How is it?) |
| haben wir | **ham wa** | Ham wa Zeit? (Do we have time?) |

> Use these only with friends - never in the exam or with your boss.

### Rule 7: The power of doch (the micro-word with many jobs)

| Job | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Contradicting a "no" | - Du kommst nicht? - **Doch!** | - You're not coming? - Yes I am! |
| Adding emphasis | Das ist **doch** super! | That's really great! |
| Soothing a request | Komm **doch** rein! | Come on in! |
| Correcting politely | Das ist **doch** nicht so schlimm. | It's really not that bad. |

> **Doch** is the one-word answer to a negative question - the word English doesn't have. Full grammar in `../02-Grammar-Core/07-grammar-essentials.md` §16.

### Rule 10: Verb-first statements (V1-Sätze) - "Mach ich dann gleich"

You learned "verb at position 2". In spoken German, the verb jumps to position 1 - like a question, but it's a statement. Germans drop the object (das) and the verb goes FIRST. Super common at work!

| Textbook (full) | Spoken (V1) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Das mache ich dann gleich. | **Mach ich dann gleich.** | I'll do it right away. |
| Das weiß ich nicht. | **Weiß ich nicht.** | I don't know. |
| Das kriege ich hin. | **Krieg ich hin.** | I'll manage it. |
| Das habe ich schon. | **Hab ich schon.** | Already have it. |
| Das brauche ich nicht. | **Brauch ich nicht.** | I don't need it. |
| Das schaffe ich. | **Schaff ich.** | I'll make it. |
| Ich komme gleich. | **Komm ich gleich.** | I'll come right away. |
| Ich rufe dich an. | **Ruf ich dich an.** | I'll call you. |

> **How it works:** drop the object (das) → verb first → ich → rest. Use it to answer quickly and casually at work: *"Kannst du das machen?" → "Mach ich gleich."*
> Friends/colleagues only - in the exam always use the full form (verb at position 2).

## 1. Honesty & Opinions - Ehrlich gesagt

| English | German (say this) | When you use it |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| To be honest... | **Ehrlich gesagt...** | Before giving your real opinion |
| Honestly, I don't know. | **Ehrlich gesagt, keine Ahnung.** (Ehrlich gesagt, ich weiß nicht.) | When you truly don't know |
| In my opinion... | **Also, ich finde...** (Meiner Meinung nach...) | Stating what you think (also = the natural opener) |
| I think so. | **Glaub schon.** (Ich glaube schon.) | Agreeing (soft) |
| I don't think so. | **Ich glaub nicht.** | Disagreeing (soft) |

| That's not right. | **Stimmt nicht.** (Das stimmt nicht.) | Correcting someone |
| I agree with you. | **Da bin ich dabei.** (Ich stimme dir zu.) | Agreement, casual |

## 6. Explaining & Clarifying - Erklären und Verstehen

| English | German (say this) | When you use it |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| I mean... | **Ich mein...** / **Also...** | Clarifying what you said |
| How do you say...? | **Wie sagt man noch mal...?** | Looking for a word |
| How do you call this? | **Wie heißt das noch mal?** | Don't know a word |
| What does this mean? | **Was heißt das?** (Was bedeutet das?) | Don't understand a word - heißen is the spoken one |
| Can you repeat that? | **Kannst du das noch mal sagen?** | Didn't catch it |
| Can you speak slower? | **Kannst du bitte langsamer sprechen?** | Too fast |
| I don't understand. | **Ich versteh das nicht.** / **Ich kapier das nicht.** (kapieren = very common spoken) | Don't understand |
| In other words... | **Also kurz gesagt...** (Mit anderen Worten...) | Rephrasing |
| For example... | **Zum Beispiel...** | Giving an example |
| Like this. | **So.** / **Einfach so.** | Demonstrating |
| Wait a moment. | **Moment mal!** / **Warte kurz!** | Need a moment |
| The thing is... / You see... | **Nämlich...** | Explaining the reason (goes AFTER the verb) |
| Thanks for the info. | **Danke für die Info!** (Danke für die Auskunft.) | After someone explains something - Info is the daily word |

**"Nämlich" - the secret weapon for explanations:**

"Nämlich" means "the thing is" or "you see" - it explains WHY. It goes AFTER the verb:

- Ich kann nicht kommen, ich muss **nämlich** arbeiten. (I can't come, the thing is I have to work.)
- Wir wollen **nämlich** draußen im Garten feiern. (You see, we want to celebrate outside in the garden.)
- Er kommt spät, er hat **nämlich** keine Zeit. (He comes late, the thing is he has no time.)

!!! example "Example conversation"
    - "Wie sagt man auf Deutsch 'to be honest'?" (How do you say 'to be honest' in German?)
    - "Ehrlich gesagt." (To be honest.)
    - "Wie bitte? Kannst du das noch mal sagen?" (Excuse me? Can you say that again?)
    - "Ehr-lich ge-sagt." (To be honest. - spoken slowly)

**The da … hin split (da = there + hin = to/towards → "dahin")**" splits in speech)

| Spoken | Meaning | Full form |
|---|---|---|
| Da brauchst du **nicht mehr** hin. | You don't need to go there anymore. | Da musst du nicht mehr hingehen. |
| Da geh ich **morgen** hin. | I'll go there tomorrow. | Dahin gehe ich morgen. |
| Da fahr ich **mit dem Auto** hin. | I'll drive there by car. | Dahin fahre ich mit dem Auto. |
| Ich muss **da** auch **hin**. | I have to go there too. | Ich muss auch dahin. |

!!! example "The daily trio (say these together!) - wegen + nicht mehr + da ... hin"
    - Ich wohne **nicht mehr** in Berlin, **wegen** der Arbeit. (I don't live in Berlin anymore, because of work.)
    - Brauchst du **da** noch **hin**? - Nein, ich muss **nicht mehr** hin. (Do you still need to go there? - No, I don't need to go anymore.)
    - Wir gehen **wegen** dem Tim zum Kindergarten, und danach **da** auch **hin**. (We go to the kindergarten because of Tim, and afterwards there too.)

**Da = the word with many jobs (don't translate it 1:1 - it depends on context!)** depends on context!)

| Job | Meaning | Example | 
|---|---|---|
| place | there | Da ist mein Buch. (My book is THERE.) · Er ist nicht da. (He's not there/here.) |
| time | then / at that moment | **Da** war ich noch jung. (I was still young THEN.) · **Da** hab ich gelacht! (I laughed at THAT point!) |
| reason | because / since | **Da** es regnet, bleibe ich zu Hause. (BECAUSE it rains, I stay home.) |
| pointing | there it is / here! | **Da!** / **Da ist er!** (There he is!) - the word you say when you point |

**The da-compounds (da + preposition = "that/it"):**

| Compound | Daily use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| dabei | in / on it | Bist du **dabei**? (Are you in?) → **Bin dabei!** |
| darauf | on it / at it | Ich freue mich **darauf**! (looking forward to it) |
| davon | of it / about it | Was hältst du **davon**? (What do you think of it?) |
| drauf | auf + da, spoken | Kommt **drauf** an. (It depends.) |
| drüber | über + da, spoken | Sprechen wir später **drüber**. (talk about it later) |

> **How to tell place vs. time:** if the sentence is about a moment, it's time + then → **Da war ich noch jung.** If it's about a location, it's place → **Das Buch ist da.** Context decides!

## 16. Seeking Agreement - Don't you think? / Right? / Isn't it?

> Germans rarely make a plain statement. They add a tiny tag at the end to check if you agree. Learn these and you sound native instantly.

| English | German (say this) | When you use it |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| Don't you think? | **Findest du nicht auch?** / **Meinst du nicht auch?** | Asking for opinion - soft, friendly |
| Don't you agree? | **Oder?** / **Nicht wahr?** | Tag at end - most common |
| Right? Isn't it? | **Stimmt's?** / **Stimmt, oder?** | Checking a fact |
| Isn't it beautiful? | **Schön, oder?** / **Schön, nicht?** | Comment + tag |
| You know? | **Weißt du?** / **Weißt du, was ich meine?** | Checking if listener follows |
| You know what I mean? | **Verstehst du, was ich meine?** | After explaining something |
| Let's say ... right? | **... , oder?** | The universal tag - put oder at END |
| Ne? (casual) | **..., ne?** | Very spoken, friends only - Das ist gut, ne? |
| Gell? (south) | **..., gell?** | Munich/Bavaria - Servus, gell? |

**How to use oder - the one-word magic:**

Just add **oder?** or **ne?** at the end of ANY statement:

- Das Wetter ist schön, **oder?** - The weather is nice, right?
- Du kommst morgen, **oder?** - You come tomorrow, right?
- Das ist teuer, **oder?** - That is expensive, isn't it?
- Wir treffen uns um 8, **oder?** - We meet at 8, right?

> **Spoken German:** oder is #1, ne is #2 (friends), nicht wahr is formal. In Munich you will hear **gell** - same as oder.

!!! example "Example conversation"

    - "Das Restaurant ist super, **oder?**" (The restaurant is great, right?)
    - "Ja, find ich auch! Meinst du nicht auch, wir sollten öfter hier essen?" (Yes, I think so too! Don't you think we should eat here more often?)
    - "Auf jeden Fall! **Stimmt's?**" (Definitely! Right?)

## 17. Hedging & Softening - I guess / Probably / Actually / In my opinion

> Direct German sounds rude in English. These softeners make you sound polite and natural. Use them BEFORE your real sentence.

| English | German (say this) | When you use it |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| I think ... | **Ich glaube, ...** / **Ich denke, ...** | Soft opinion - Ich glaube, das ist gut. |
| I guess ... | **Ich schätze, ...** / **Vermutlich ...** | Not 100% sure |
| Probably | **Wahrscheinlich** / **Vermutlich** | 70% sure - Wahrscheinlich kommt er später. |
| Maybe | **Vielleicht** / **Vielleicht, ja.** | 50% sure |
| Actually / In fact | **Eigentlich ...** | Correcting / revealing - Eigentlich mag ich keinen Kaffee. |
| To be honest | **Ehrlich gesagt, ...** | Before real opinion (you know this) |
| In my opinion | **Meiner Meinung nach ...** / **Ich finde, ...** | Formal vs spoken - Ich finde, das ist teuer. |
| As far as I know | **Soweit ich weiß, ...** | Not fully sure |
| If you ask me | **Wenn du mich fragst, ...** | Giving personal view |
| Honestly | **Ganz ehrlich, ...** | Stronger than ehrlich gesagt |

**The softener position - put it at START, then main sentence:**e:

- **Eigentlich** mag ich keinen Kaffee, aber dieser hier ist gut. - Actually I do not like coffee, but this one is good.
- **Wahrscheinlich** komme ich später. - Probably I will come later.
- **Vielleicht** gehen wir morgen ins Kino. - Maybe we go to the cinema tomorrow.
- **Ich glaube,** das ist zu teuer. - I think that is too expensive.
- **Meiner Meinung nach** ist das Restaurant super. - In my opinion the restaurant is great.

> **A1 trick:** start with **Ich finde,** - it works for EVERY opinion and is always correct: **Ich finde, das ist gut. / Ich finde, das ist zu teuer. / Ich finde, München ist schön.

!!! example "Example conversation"

    - "Kommst du morgen?" (Do you come tomorrow?)
    - "**Wahrscheinlich,** aber ich bin mir nicht sicher. **Eigentlich** habe ich keine Zeit, **aber vielleicht** schaffe ich es." (Probably, but I'm not sure. Actually I have no time, but maybe I will make it.)
    - "**Meinst du nicht auch,** das ist zu viel Stress?" (Don't you think that is too much stress?)
