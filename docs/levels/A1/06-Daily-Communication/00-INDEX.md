## What you can do after this Part

- Soften requests with mal (Kannst du mir mal helfen?).
- Sound human with fillers (naja, halt, eben, irgendwie).
- Use V1 statements at work (Mach ich gleich.).
- Check agreement with oder? / Stimmt's? / Findest du nicht auch?

## Tip

This is the most important Part for B1 transition. The grammar is correct, but the STYLE is spoken. Use 01-daily-conversations as your daily phrasebook, not as a reading book.
