# Writing Survival - Schreiben Überleben (telc A1)

> **Why this file exists:** In the exam you have about 15 minutes for the 2 writing tasks (the written part is 45 minutes total, shared with Lesen). This file gives you every phrase, every template, every shortcut. Open this file, find what you need, write it. No thinking required - just copy, adjust, done.

---

## The Exam in 30 Seconds

| Task | What you get | What you write | Points |
|------|-------------|----------------|--------|
| **Task 1: Formular** | Empty boxes with labels | Your personal data | ~7 of the 15 |
| **Task 2: Mitteilung** | One sentence prompt | Short message (official: ca. 30 Wörter) | ~8 of the 15 |
| **Total (Schreiben)** | | | 15 points (of the 60 exam points) |

**Passing rule:** Write something in every box, write the message. ==Empty = 0 points.== (Pass = 36 of 60 overall, no minimum per section.)

### Date format

## TASK 2: The Message - Mitteilung

### The skeleton (use for EVERY message)

```
[Anrede],
[2-3 Sätze: Situation + Wunsch/Aussage].
[Grußformel]
[Name]
```

### Anrede (Greeting) - pick the right one

| Situation | German | When to use |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Friend / peer | **Liebe Anna,** | female friend |
| Friend / peer | **Lieber Thomas,** | male friend |
| Informal group | **Hallo zusammen,** | group of friends |
| Formal / stranger | **Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,** | official letter |
| Formal (known name) | **Sehr geehrter Herr Weber,** | male authority |
| Formal (known name) | **Sehr geehrte Frau Schmidt,** | female authority |
| Super informal | **Hey,** | close friend (risky in exam) |

### Grußformel (Closing) - pick the right one

| Situation | German | When to use |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Friendly (standard) | **Viele Grüße** | most messages |
| Warm | **Liebe Grüße** | friends, family |
| Formal | **Mit freundlichen Grüßen** | official letters |
| Warm + name | **Herzliche Grüße** | close friends |
| Quick | **Bis bald!** | see you soon |
| Quick | **Bis Montag!** | see you Monday |

### Signature

- First name only: **Hamed** (friends)
- Full name: **Hamed Okhovvat** (formal)

## THE 7 TEMPLATES (cover 95% of exam tasks)

### Template 1: Invitation - Einladung

    ```

    ich habe am Samstag Geburtstag. Möchtest du zu meiner Party kommen?
    Die Party ist um 19 Uhr bei mir zu Hause.

    Viele Grüße
    Hamed
    ```

## EMERGENCY EXPRESSIONS

When you don't know what to write, use these:

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Don't know a word | Use a simpler word or describe it: "Das Ding zum Schreiben" (Stift) |
| Don't know the grammar | Use short simple sentences: "Ich komme nicht. Ich bin krank." |
| Running out of time | Write the closing first: "Viele Grüße, Hamed" then add sentences before |
| Don't understand the prompt | Look for key words: Einladung, Entschuldigung, Dank, Termin |
| Too many mistakes | Cross out and rewrite - don't try to fix every error |
| Don't know the date format | Write: 21. September 1985 (words, not numbers) |

## MISTAKES TO AVOID

| ❌ Don't write | ✅ Write instead | Why |
|----------------|------------------|-----|
| Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, ich habe... | Liebe Anna, ich habe... | "Sehr geehrte" is too formal for friends |
| Ich hatte Geburtstag | Ich habe Geburtstag | Present tense, not past |
| Wir treffen am Montag uns | Wir treffen uns am Montag | Verb at end after time phrase |
| Ich bin krank. Ich komme nicht. | Ich bin krank und kann nicht kommen. | Combine with "und" |
| Liebe Grüße Hamed Okhovvat | Viele Grüße Hamed | Keep it simple and short |
| (empty Task 1 boxes) | Fill every box | Empty = 0 points |
