# Grammar Terms - The Words This Book Uses (A1)

> German grammar has its own vocabulary, and this book uses it. You do not need to memorize this page - keep it open next to your lessons for the first weeks, and look a term up here instead of guessing. Every term below is explained in full where it first appears in the lessons; this page is your quick dictionary.

---

## 1. The sentence

| Term | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Satz | sentence | Ich lerne Deutsch. |
| Subjekt | the person/thing the sentence is about (the "who/what" that does or is something) | **Ich** lerne Deutsch. |
| Verb | the doing/being word | Ich **lerne** Deutsch. |
| Objekt | the person/thing the verb affects | Ich lerne **Deutsch**. |
| Adjektiv | describing word | ein **neues** Auto |
| Adverb | word that says when/how/where | **heute**, **gern**, **hier** |
| Präposition | small word before a noun (in, auf, mit, zu) | **in** der Stadt |

!!! note "Subjekt is not always 'the doer'"
    In "Das Buch gefällt mir" the subject is das Buch, but nobody does anything - the book pleases me. The safe test: the verb agrees with the subject (Das Buch **gefällt**... not gefallen).

## 2. Nouns and their little words

| Term | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Nomen / Substantiv | noun - a person, thing, or idea | der Mann, die Stadt |
| Artikel | the little word before a noun | der/die/das, ein/eine |
| Genus | gender - every noun is m/f/n | der (m), die (f), das (n) |
| Singular / Plural | one / more than one | das Kind / die Kinder |
| Kasus / Fall | the case - the job the noun does in the sentence | Nominativ, Akkusativ, Dativ |
| Nominativ | subject case | **Der** Mann hilft. |
| Akkusativ | direct-object case (who/what is affected) | Ich sehe **den** Mann. |
| Dativ | indirect-object case (to/for whom) | Ich helfe **dem** Mann. |
| Genitiv | possession case ("of") | das Auto **des** Mannes |
| Possessivartikel | my, your, his, ... | **mein**, **dein**, **sein** |
| Demonstrativpronomen | pointing word: this/that | **dieser** Mann |

## 3. Verbs

| Term | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| konjugieren | change the verb for the person | ich lerne, du lernst |
| Infinitiv | the dictionary form (to ...) | lernen, gehen |
| Modalverb | verb that changes the mood (can, must, want, would like) | können, müssen, wollen, möchten |
| Hilfsverb | helper verb for past tenses | haben, sein (ich **habe** gelernt) |
| Partizip II | the past form used with haben/sein | gelernt, gegangen |
| trennbares Verb | separable verb - prefix goes to the end | **auf**stehen: Ich stehe um 7 Uhr **auf**. |
| reflexives Verb | verb with mich/dich/sich (action comes back to you) | sich freuen: Ich freue **mich**. |
| Imperativ | command form | Komm! / Kommen Sie! |

## 4. Tenses

| Term | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Präsens | present tense | ich lerne |
| Perfekt | spoken past (helper + Partizip II) | ich habe gelernt |
| Präteritum | past form used for sein/haben/modals and narration | ich war, ich hatte, ich konnte |
| Futur | future with werden + Infinitiv | ich werde lernen |
| Konjunktiv II | polite/would form | ich hätte, ich würde |

## 5. Building sentences

| Term | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Konjunktion | joining word (and, but, because) | und, aber, weil |
| Nebensatz | clause whose verb goes to the end | weil ich müde **bin** |
| Relativsatz | clause with der/die/das = who/which | der Mann, der dort steht |
| Komparativ / Superlativ | bigger / biggest | größer, am größten |
| Wechselpräposition | two-way preposition (Wo? = Dativ, Wohin? = Akkusativ) | auf dem Tisch / auf den Tisch |
| n-Deklination | masculine nouns that add -n (A2) | der Junge - den Jungen |
| Negation | saying no | kein, nicht |
