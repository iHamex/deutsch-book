# Pronunciation and Spelling - How German Sounds (A1)

> German spelling is almost regular: once you know the letter sounds, you can read most words correctly even if you have never heard them. This page gives you the sounds you need for listening, for reading new words aloud, and for spelling your name in the exam. Say everything out loud - reading alone does not train the ear.

---

## 1. The vowels

| Letter | Sound (English clue) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a | "ah" (father) | Tag, Name |
| e | "eh" (bed, slightly longer) | lesen, zehn |
| i | "ee" (see) | bin, vier |
| o | "oh" (more) | wo, rot |
| u | "oo" (moon) | du, gut |

**Vowel length matters.** A long vowel is held longer (Tag = "taahk"); a short vowel is clipped (Mann = "mahn"). Rule of thumb: before one consonant or h the vowel is usually long; before a double consonant it is short.

| Short | Long |
|---|---|
| Mann, Bett, Kind, Gott, Hund | Tag, sehr, vier, rot, gut |

Train with pairs: Stadt (short) vs Staat (long), Kamm (short) vs kam (long). Hear the difference in the audio of the official telc Übungstest.

## 2. The umlauts - the heart of German

| Letter | How to say it | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| ä | like "e" in bed, but with an a-colour | Männer, spät | men, late |
| ö | round your lips and say "eh" | schön, hören | beautiful, to hear |
| ü | round your lips and say "ee" | über, fünf | over, five |

!!! warning "The umlaut changes the meaning"
    schon (already) vs schön (beautiful), Mutter (mother) vs Mütter (mothers), spät (late) vs Staat (state). In listening tasks the umlaut IS the answer - hear it.

When spelling: Ä = "A-Umlaut", Ö = "O-Umlaut", Ü = "U-Umlaut" (see [01-numbers-dates.md section 9](01-numbers-dates.md#9-spelling-buchstabieren)).

## 3. ß and ss

| Written | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ß | sharp s, long vowel before it | Straße, heiß, groß |
| ss | sharp s, short vowel before it | Wasser, dass, muss |

ß = "Eszett". When spelling: "Straße = S-T-R-A-ß-E, das ß ist Eszett."

---

## 4. The tricky consonants

| Letters | Sound | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| ch | after e/i/ä/ö/ü: soft, like "h" in huge | ich, Milch | I, milk |
| ch | after a/o/u: hard, like "ch" in Scottish loch | Nacht, Buch | night, book |
| sch | "sh" | Schule, Tisch | school, table |
| sp / st | at word start: "shp" / "sht" | Sprache, Stadt | language, city |
| z | "ts" | Zeit, zwei | time, two |
| w | "v" | was, wo | what, where |
| v | "f" in native words | vier, viel | four, much |
| j | "y" | ja, jung | yes, young |
| ei | "eye" | nein, zwei | no, two |
| ie | "ee" | nie, Liebe | never, love |

!!! tip "ei vs ie"
    ei = "eye" (mein, zwei). ie = "ee" (die, Liebe). The second letter is the one you say.

---

## 5. Word stress

- Simple words: stress the FIRST syllable - **Ar**beit, **ler**nen, **Müt**ter.
- Compounds: stress the FIRST part - **Wo**chenende, **Fuß**ball, **Bah**nhof.
- A few everyday words stress the last syllable - Kaf**fee**, Ho**tel**, Bü**ro**.
- Rule of thumb: first syllable for most words; when in doubt, follow the audio.

---

## 6. Sentence intonation

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Statement | falling at the end | Ich heiße Hamed. (falls) |
| W-question | falling | Wo wohnen Sie? (falls) |
| Yes/No question | rising at the end | Kommen Sie aus dem Iran? (rises) |

---

## 7. Punctuation and capitalization - two rules that matter for the exam

- **Nouns are capitalized** - der Mann, die Stadt, das Kind. Every noun, always. This is not optional in German.
- **German comma rule at A1:** a comma separates a weil/dass/wenn-clause from the main clause - Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil es regnet. See [01-word-order.md section 6](../04-Sentence-Building/01-word-order.md#6-subordinate-clauses-verb-goes-to-the-end).

## 8. Practice - spell and read aloud

- Spell your name and city: H-A-M-E-D / M-Ü-N-C-H-E-N (Ü = "U-Umlaut").
- Read aloud: Straße, schön, fünf, Zeit, Sprache, Nacht, Milch, Brot, Tschüss.
- Say the pairs and hear the difference: schon/schön, Stadt/Staat, Mutter/Mütter.
- Ask someone (or the audio) to spell a word; write what you hear.

??? success "Antworten - check the sounds"

    1. Straße = S-T-R-A-ß-E (ß = Eszett).
    2. Tschüss = T-S-C-H-Ü-S-S (Ü = U-Umlaut).
    3. schon (already) vs schön (beautiful) - the umlaut changes the meaning.
    4. ei = "eye" (nein), ie = "ee" (Liebe).
