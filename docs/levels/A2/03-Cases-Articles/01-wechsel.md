# Wechselpräpositionen - Two-Way Prepositions (A2)

> At A1 you learned the basic rule: Wohin? = Accusative. Wo? = Dative. At A2 you master the position pairs (liegen/legen, stehen/stellen, sitzen/setzen, hängen) and use them in real sentences.

---

## 1. The nine Wechselpräpositionen

**in, an, auf, vor, hinter, über, unter, neben, zwischen**

| Preposition | Wo? (Dative = location) | Wohin? (Accusative = movement) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **in** | in (inside) | into (into) |
| **an** | at/on (side) | to/at (to side) |
| **auf** | on (surface) | onto (onto surface) |
| **vor** | in front of (location) | to front of (movement) |
| **hinter** | behind (location) | to behind (movement) |
| **über** | above/over (location) | over to (movement) |
| **unter** | under/underneath (location) | under/underneath (movement) |
| **neben** | next to/beside (location) | to next to (movement) |
| **zwischen** | between (location) | between (movement) |

---

## 2. Wo? = Dative (where is it?)

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Das Buch liegt **auf dem** Tisch. | The book lies on the table. |
| Das Bild hängt **an der** Wand. | The picture hangs on the wall. |
| Er steht **vor der** Tür. | He stands in front of the door. |
| Das Auto steht **hinter dem** Haus. | The car stands behind the house. |
| Die Lampe hängt **über dem** Tisch. | The lamp hangs above the table. |
| Die Katze liegt **unter dem** Stuhl. | The cat lies under the chair. |
| Ich sitze **neben dem** Fenster. | I sit next to the window. |
| Er sitzt **zwischen mir und** dir. | He sits between me and you. |

---

## 3. Wohin? = Accusative (where to?)

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ich lege das Buch **auf den** Tisch. | I put the book onto the table. |
| Ich hänge das Bild **an die** Wand. | I hang the picture on the wall. |
| Er stellt sich **vor die** Tür. | He positions himself in front of the door. |
| Er stellt das Auto **hinter das** Haus. | He puts the car behind the house. |
| Ich stelle die Lampe **über den** Tisch. | I put the lamp above the table. |
| Er stellt sich **unter den** Baum. | He positions himself under the tree. |
| Ich stelle mich **neben die** Tür. | I position myself next to the door. |
| Er stellt den Stuhl **zwischen die** Stühle. | He puts the chair between the chairs. |

---

## 4. The position pairs - liegen/legen, stehen/stellen, sitzen/setzen

These pairs are the KEY to Wechselpräpositionen at A2:

| Verb | Meaning | Wo? (Dative) | Wohin? (Accusative) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **liegen** | to lie (location) | Das Buch **liegt auf dem** Tisch. | - |
| **legen** | to put (movement) | - | Ich **lege** das Buch **auf den** Tisch. |
| **stehen** | to stand (location) | Die Vase **steht auf dem** Schrank. | - |
| **stellen** | to put upright (movement) | - | Ich **stelle** die Vase **auf den** Schrank. |
| **sitzen** | to sit (location) | Er **sitzt auf dem** Stuhl. | - |
| **setzen** | to sit down (movement) | - | Er **setzt sich auf den** Stuhl. |
| **hängen** | to hang (location) | Das Bild **hängt an der** Wand. | - |
| **hängen** | to hang (movement) | - | Ich **hänge** das Bild **an die** Wand. |

!!! warning "liegen vs legen, stehen vs stellen"
    liegen/stehen/sitzen = where IS it? (Dative, location). legen/stellen/setzen = where DO you PUT it? (Accusative, movement).
    - Das Buch **liegt** auf dem Tisch. (where it is)
    - Ich **lege** das Buch auf den Tisch. (where I put it)

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Das Buch liegt auf dem Tisch.* / *Ich lege das Buch auf den Tisch.* (The book lies on the table. / I put the book on the table.)

## 5. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wo? (location) | Dative | Das Buch liegt auf dem Tisch. | The book lies on the table. |
| Wohin? (movement) | Accusative | Ich lege das Buch auf den Tisch. | I put the book on the table. |
| liegen/legen | horizontal | Das Buch liegt/lege ich auf den Tisch. | The book lies/I put it on the table. |
| stehen/stellen | vertical | Die Vase steht/stelle ich auf den Schrank. | The vase stands/I put it on the shelf. |
| sitzen/setzen | sitting | Er sitzt/setzt sich auf den Stuhl. | He sits/he sits down on the chair. |

- [ ] Wo? = Dative. Wohin? = Accusative.
- [ ] liegen/legen (horizontal), stehen/stellen (vertical), sitzen/setzen (sitting).
- [ ] hängen works for both (same form).

## 6. Practice - Wo or Wohin?

Decide: location (Dative) or movement (Accusative)? Then say the full sentence.

- The book lies on the table. (Wo)
- I put the book on the table. (Wohin)
- The picture hangs on the wall. (Wo)
- I hang the picture on the wall. (Wohin)
- He stands in front of the door. (Wo)
- He positions himself in front of the door. (Wohin)
- The cat lies under the chair. (Wo)
- The car stands behind the house. (Wo)
- I sit next to the window. (Wo)
- He puts the chair between the chairs. (Wohin)
- She sits down on the chair. (Wohin)
- The vase stands on the cupboard. (Wo)
