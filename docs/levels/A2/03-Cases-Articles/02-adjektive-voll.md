# Full Adjective Declension (A2)

> At A1 you learned adjective endings after ein-word (eine neue Wohnung) and after no article (neue Wohnung). At A2 you master ALL three systems: after der-word, after ein-word, and after no article - in all three cases. This is the most-examined grammar point at A2 (Adjektivdeklination appears in Lesen, Schreiben, and Sprechen).

---

## 1. The three systems

| System | Article type | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **der-word** | der, die, das, dieser, jeder, welcher, alle, beide, mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, kein | **der neue** Mann |
| **ein-word** | ein, eine, kein, mein, dein, sein, ihr... (with an ending) | **ein neuer** Mann |
| **no article** | bare noun | **neuer** Mann |

The idea: the adjective shows the case ONLY where the article does not. If the article already shows the case strongly (der, dem, den, die), the adjective stays weak (-e or -en). If the article shows nothing (ein, no article), the adjective takes a strong ending (-er, -es, -e, -en, -em).

---

## 2. der-word endings (weak declension)

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nom** | der alt**e** Mann | die alt**e** Frau | das alt**e** Kind | die alt**en** Kinder |
| **Akk** | den alt**en** Mann | die alt**e** Frau | das alt**e** Kind | die alt**en** Kinder |
| **Dat** | dem alt**en** Mann | der alt**en** Frau | dem alt**en** Kind | den alt**en** Kindern |

!!! tip "der-word rule"
    After der-words there are only TWO endings: **-e** (Nom masc/fem/neut, Akk fem/neut) and **-en** (everything else: Akk masc, all Dative, all Plural).

### der-word examples in sentences

| Example | Case | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Der neue** Mann ist nett. | Nom | The new man is nice. |
| Ich kenne **den neuen** Mann. | Akk | I know the new man. |
| Ich helfe **dem neuen** Mann. | Dat | I help the new man. |
| **Die kleine** Frau kommt. | Nom | The small woman is coming. |
| Ich sehe **die kleine** Frau. | Akk | I see the small woman. |
| Das ist das Auto **des neuen** Mannes. | Gen | That is the new man's car. |

## 3. ein-word endings (mixed declension)

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nom** | ein neu**er** Mann | eine neu**e** Frau | ein neu**es** Kind | (meine) neu**en** Kinder |
| **Akk** | einen neu**en** Mann | eine neu**e** Frau | ein neu**es** Kind | (meine) neu**en** Kinder |
| **Dat** | einem neu**en** Mann | einer neu**en** Frau | einem neu**en** Kind | (meinen) neu**en** Kindern |

!!! tip "ein-word rule"
    ein/eine/ein show NO gender in Nom masc and Nom/Akk neut - so the adjective carries it: ein neu**er** Mann, ein neu**es** Kind. Everywhere else the ending is **-e** or **-en** exactly like the der-word table.

### ein-word examples in sentences

| Example | Case | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ein neuer** Mann kommt. | Nom | A new man is coming. |
| Ich sehe **einen neuen** Mann. | Akk | I see a new man. |
| Ich helfe **einem neuen** Mann. | Dat | I help a new man. |
| Sie kauft **eine teure** Tasche. | Akk | She buys an expensive bag. |
| Er trinkt **ein kaltes** Bier. | Akk | He drinks a cold beer. |
| Ich suche **eine neue** Wohnung. | Akk | I am looking for a new apartment. (exam sentence!) |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Ich suche eine neue Wohnung.* (I am looking for a new apartment.) / *Ich möchte einen großen Kaffee.* (I would like a large coffee.)

## 4. No article (strong declension)

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nom** | neu**er** Kaffee | neu**e** Milch | neu**es** Wasser | neu**e** Schuhe |
| **Akk** | neu**en** Kaffee | neu**e** Milch | neu**es** Wasser | neu**e** Schuhe |
| **Dat** | neu**em** Kaffee | neu**er** Milch | neu**em** Wasser | neu**en** Schuhen |

!!! tip "no article rule"
    With no article the adjective shows the case itself - like ein does. Nom masc = -er, Dat = -em, Dat fem = -er, Dat plural = -en. You hear this most with **mass nouns** (no plural): Ich trinke gern **heißen Tee** (Akk), mit **frischem Brot** (Dat).

### no-article examples in real sentences

| Example | Case | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ich trinke gern **heißen** Tee. | Akk | I like drinking hot tea. |
| Mit **frischem** Brot schmeckt es besser. | Dat | With fresh bread it tastes better. |
| Ich habe **keine** Zeit. | (kein = ein-word) | I have no time. |
| Ich brauche **kaltes** Wasser. | Akk | I need cold water. |

!!! warning "No article is rare in speech"
    In everyday speech you almost always use an article: Ich trinke gern **einen heißen** Tee. The no-article table matters mostly for **mass nouns** (kein plural, no ein): Ich habe **keinen Hunger** uses keinen (ein-word!). Use the no-article forms mainly after: ohne, als, and with uncountables.

## 5. The four endings that decide everything

| Ending | Appears when | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **-e** | after der/die/das: Nom (all genders, sing), Akk fem/neut | weak |
| **-en** | after dem/den/der (Dat): Akk masc, ALL Dative, ALL Plural | weak |
| **-er / -es** | after ein/mein/kein where the article shows nothing: Nom masc (-er), Nom/Akk neut (-es) | mixed/strong |
| **-er / -e / -es / -em / -en** | no article at all: the adjective IS the article | strong |

!!! warning "Dative is always -en after an article"
    After dem, der (Dat fem), einem, einer, meinem... every adjective ends in **-en**. No exceptions: mit dem neu**en** Auto, in einer klein**en** Stadt, zu einem teur**en** Restaurant.

## 6. The one-table method - match the article ending

Instead of memorizing three tables, copy the article's own ending onto the adjective when the article is "empty":

| Article | Article shows | Adjective takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| der / die / das | everything | -e (Nom) or -en |
| dem / den / der (Dat) | everything | -en |
| ein (masc Nom) | nothing | -er |
| ein (neut Nom/Akk) | nothing | -es |
| eine (fem) | shows -e | -e |
| einen | shows -en | -en |
| kein article | nothing | full ending (-er, -es, -e, -em, -en) |

!!! tip "The practical test"
    Look at the word before the adjective. Does it already end in -er/-es/-em/-en? Then the adjective stays **-e/-en** (weak). Does it end in nothing useful (ein, kein ending)? Then the adjective carries the strong ending itself.

## 8. Cheat sheet

| System | Nom masc | Nom neut | Akk masc | Dat (any) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| der-word | -e | -e | -en | -en |
| ein-word | -er | -es | -en | -en |
| no article | -er | -es | -en | -em (masc/neut) |

- [ ] Dative = always -en after an article.
- [ ] ein (masc) and ein (neut) show nothing -> adjective takes -er / -es.
- [ ] Strong endings only when the article is empty or missing.
- [ ] Practice aloud: eine neue Wohnung, einen großen Kaffee, ein kaltes Bier.
