# Genitive - Names and Formal Text (A2, Receptive-Active)

> At A1 you used von + Dativ for possession (das Auto von Thomas). At A2 you learn the Genitive: for names (Thomas' Auto, Annas Buch) and for recognizing genitive prepositions (wegen, trotz, während) when you read them. You produce the name forms and the von + Dativ forms; you recognize the rest.

---

## 1. Genitive for names - the form you actually use

For people's names, add **-s**:

| Name | Genitive | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anna | Annas | Das ist **Annas** Buch. | That is Anna's book. |
| Hamed | Hameds | **Hameds** Wohnung ist klein. | Hamed's apartment is small. |
| Thomas | Thomas' | Das ist **Thomas'** Auto. | That is Thomas's car. |
| Felix | Felix' | Ich kenne **Felix'** Bruder. | I know Felix's brother. |

!!! warning "After -s, -ss, -x, -z, -tz only an apostrophe"
    Names ending in -s/-x/-z take ONLY an apostrophe, never -s: **Thomas'** Auto, **Felix'** Bruder, **Sina's** is wrong (Sina -> **Sinas**). You write Thomas' but you say it like Thomas.

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Das ist Annas Buch.* / *Das ist Thomas' Auto.* (That is Anna's book. / That is Thomas's car.)

---

## 2. Genitive for common nouns (receptive - read it)

For common nouns, der-word articles change in Genitive, and masculine/neuter nouns add -(e)s:

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nom** | der Mann | die Frau | das Kind | die Kinder |
| **Gen** | des Mann**es** | der Frau | des Kind**es** | der Kinder |

- der Preis **des Autos** (the price of the car)
- die Tasche **der Frau** (the woman's bag)
- das Ende **des Tages** (the end of the day)
- die Farbe **der Wände** (the color of the walls)

!!! warning "Genitive masc/neut = des + noun + -(e)s"
    des Mannes, des Kindes, des Tisches. One-syllable nouns usually take -es (des Tages), longer nouns take -s (des Autos). Feminine and plural never add -s.

---

## 3. von + Dativ is your speaking default

In spoken German, possession is almost always von + Dativ:

| Genitive (formal, written) | von + Dativ (spoken - use this) | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| das Auto **Thomas'** | das Auto **von Thomas** | Thomas's car |
| das Buch **der Lehrerin** | das Buch **von der Lehrerin** | the teacher's book |
| die Wohnung **meines Freundes** | die Wohnung **von meinem Freund** | my friend's apartment |

!!! tip "Your A2 rule"
    Speak: **von + Dativ** always works and never sounds wrong. Names in -s (Thomas' Auto) are also everyday spoken German. The full noun Genitive (das Auto des Mannes) stays for writing and reading.

---

## 4. Genitive prepositions (receptive - read them)

These prepositions take Genitive in formal writing; in speech people often use Dativ:

| Preposition | Meaning | Formal (Gen) | Spoken (Dat) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **wegen** | because of | wegen **des Wetters** | wegen **dem Wetter** |
| **trotz** | despite | trotz **des Regens** | trotz **dem Regen** |
| **während** | during | während **des Urlaubs** | während **dem Urlaub** |
| **statt** | instead of | statt **des Buches** | statt **dem Buch** |
| **außerhalb** | outside of | außerhalb **der Stadt** | außerhalb **der Stadt** |
| **innerhalb** | within | innerhalb **des Gebäudes** | innerhalb **dem Gebäude** |

!!! warning "wegen takes Genitive in writing, Dativ in speech"
    wegen **des** Wetters (formal, exam text). wegen **dem** Wetter (spoken - what Germans actually say). Both are correct German. At A2: understand both, write von + Dativ, and recognize wegen/trotz/während when you read them.

## 5. Reading practice - genitive you will see in Lesen

| Example | English | What is genitive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Am Ende **des Tages** ist er müde. | At the end of the day he is tired. | des Tages |
| Wegen **des Regens** bleibt alles geschlossen. | Because of the rain everything stays closed. | des Regens |
| Trotz **des Lärms** schläft das Kind. | Despite the noise the child sleeps. | des Lärms |
| Während **des Gesprächs** klingelt das Telefon. | During the conversation the phone rings. | des Gesprächs |
| Die Kinder **der Nachbarin** spielen im Garten. | The neighbor's children play in the garden. | der Nachbarin |

## 7. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| names | add -s (-s/-x/-z: apostrophe only) | Annas Buch / Thomas' Auto | Anna's book / Thomas's car |
| von + Dat (speaking) | default possession | Das Auto von Thomas. | Thomas's car. |
| noun Genitive (reading) | des Mannes, der Frau | das Ende des Tages | the end of the day |
| genitive prepositions | formal writing | wegen des Wetters | because of the weather |
| spoken equivalent | Dativ | wegen dem Wetter | because of the weather |

- [ ] Names: add -s; after -s/-x/-z only an apostrophe.
- [ ] von + Dativ = your speaking default.
- [ ] wegen/trotz/während take Genitive in writing, Dativ in speech.
