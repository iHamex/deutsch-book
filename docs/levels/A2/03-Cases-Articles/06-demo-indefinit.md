# Demonstratives and Indefinites (A2)

> At A1 you learned dieser, jed-, manch-, viel-, wenig-. At A2 you master the full system: der as demonstrative (der da), jed- in all forms, and the complete etwas/nichts/alles/einiges/set pieces.

---

## 1. der/die/das as demonstrative (that one)

When pointing at something, der/die/das can mean "that one" without a noun:

| Gender | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| der (m) | **Der** da ist mein Bruder. | That one there is my brother. |
| die (f) | **Die** da ist meine Schwester. | That one there is my sister. |
| das (n) | **Das** da ist mein Auto. | That one there is my car. |

With **da** or **dort** added for emphasis:

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| **Der** da ist mein Chef. | That one there is my boss. |
| **Die** dort ist die Lehrerin. | That one over there is the teacher. |
| **Das** da gefällt mir. | I like that one there. |

---

## 2. dieser/diese/dieses (this one)

dieser works as demonstrative AND as "this":

| Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **dieser** (m) | **Dieser** da ist teuer. | This one here is expensive. |
| **diese** (f) | **Diese** da ist schön. | This one here is beautiful. |
| **dieses** (n) | **Dieses** da kostet 5 Euro. | This one here costs 5 euros. |

As pronoun (without noun):

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| **Dieser** hier ist gut, **jener** dort ist schlecht. | This one here is good, that one there is bad. |

---

## 3. jed- (every/each)

| Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **jeder** (m) | **Jeder** Tag ist anders. | Every day is different. |
| **jede** (f) | **Jede** Woche rufe ich an. | Every week I call. |
| **jedes** (n) | **Jedes** Buch ist interessant. | Every book is interesting. |
| **jeden** (m Akk) | Ich lese **jeden** Tag. | I read every day. |
| **jedem** (m Dat) | Ich helfe **jedem**. | I help everyone. |

!!! tip "jeder declines like a der-word"
    jed- takes the same endings as der-words. jeder/eine/jedes in Nom, jeden in Akk masc, jedem in Dat.

---

## 4. etwas, nichts, alles, einiges, vieles

These neuter indefinites take the same endings as no-article adjectives:

| Pronoun | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **etwas** (something) | Ich brauche **etwas Neues**. | I need something new. |
| **nichts** (nothing) | Ich sehe **nichts Interessantes**. | I see nothing interesting. |
| **alles** (everything) | **Alles** klar! | Everything clear! |
| **einiges** (some things) | Ich habe **einiges** zu tun. | I have some things to do. |
| **vieles** (much/many things) | **Vieles** hat sich geändert. | Much has changed. |

!!! warning "etwas/nichts/alles take -es adjective ending"
    etwas **Neues**, nichts **Interessantes**, alles **Gute**. Always neuter, always -es.

---

## 5. manch-, viel-, wenig- review

| Pronoun | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **mancher** (m) | **Mancher** Student lernt fleißig. | Some students learn diligently. |
| **manche** (f) | **Manche** Leute sprechen Deutsch. | Some people speak German. |
| **manches** (n) | **Manches** ist schwer. | Some things are hard. |
| **viele** (pl) | **Viele** Menschen wohnen hier. | Many people live here. |
| **wenige** (pl) | **Wenige** Leute kommen. | Few people come. |
| **ein paar** (a few) | Ich habe **ein paar** Fragen. | I have a few questions. |

## 6. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| der (demonstrative) | that one | Der da ist mein Bruder. | That one there is my brother. |
| dieser | this one | Dieser hier ist gut. | This one here is good. |
| jed- | every/each | Jeder Tag ist anders. | Every day is different. |
| etwas | something | Etwas Neues. | Something new. |
| nichts | nothing | Nichts Interessantes. | Nothing interesting. |
| alles | everything | Alles klar! | Everything clear! |
| manch- | some (singular) | Mancher Student. | Some students. |
| viel- | many | Viele Menschen. | Many people. |

- [ ] der/der/das as demonstrative = that one (with da/dort).
- [ ] jed- declines like der-words.
- [ ] etwas/nichts/alles + -es adjective ending.
