## What you can do after this Part

- Tell a story in the past: Als ich klein war, spielte ich jeden Tag Fußball.
- Use difficult participles: Ich habe das vergessen / Er hat mir geholfen (review) / Sie hat es mitgebracht.
- Be polite: Ich hätte gern ein Ticket / Würden Sie mir bitte helfen?
- Guess the present: Er wird schon zu Hause sein.

## Timed Practice (weekly, 20-25 min)

1. Set a timer for 20-25 minutes.
2. Do the Practice drill in [prateritum.md section 9](01-prateritum.md#9-practice-say-it-in-prateritum) (17 items).
3. Do the Practice drill in [konjunktiv-II.md section 6](05-konjunktiv-II.md#6-practice-be-polite) (15 items).
4. Narrate your weekend aloud for 1 minute in Präteritum - no notes, timer on. Do it TWICE.
5. Self-score: count correct answers in both drills.

!!! warning "Self-score rule"
    If less than 80% correct on the drills, mark the 5 hardest items, wait 24 hours, and re-do exactly those 5.

- [ ] 2 drills done within time.

## Tip

Präteritum is for NARRATION (stories, reports). Perfekt is for CONVERSATION (what you did). At A2 you need both - the exam tests both.
