# Relative Clauses - Describing Precisely (A2)

> Relative clauses describe a noun more precisely: Das ist der Mann, der dort steht. (That is the man who stands there.) At A2 you need der/die/das in Nominative, Accusative, and Dative - and you know all three cases from A1.

---

## 1. The relative pronoun - matches the noun

The relative pronoun replaces the article of the noun it describes:

| Noun gender | Pronoun (Nom) | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| der Mann (m) | **der** | Das ist der Mann, **der** dort steht. | That is the man who stands there. |
| die Frau (f) | **die** | Das ist die Frau, **die** ich kenne. | That is the woman who I know. |
| das Kind (n) | **das** | Das ist das Kind, **das** spielt. | That is the child who plays. |
| die Kinder (pl) | **die** | Das sind die Kinder, **die** kommen. | Those are the children who come. |

---

## 2. The relative pronoun changes with CASE

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nominative** (who/which is the subject) | der | die | das | die | Der Mann, **der** dort steht, ist mein Vater. |
| **Accusative** (who/which is the object) | den | die | das | die | Der Mann, **den** ich kenne, ist nett. |
| **Dative** (to whom/which) | dem | der | dem | denen | Der Mann, **dem** ich geholfen habe, ist nett. |

### Nominative (subject of the relative clause)

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Das ist der Mann, **der** dort steht. | That is the man who stands there. |
| Das ist die Frau, **die** kommt. | That is the woman who comes. |
| Das ist das Kind, **das** spielt. | That is the child who plays. |

### Accusative (object of the relative clause)

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Das ist der Mann, **den** ich kenne. | That is the man whom I know. |
| Das ist die Frau, **die** ich sehe. | That is the woman whom I see. |
| Das ist das Buch, **das** ich lese. | That is the book which I read. |

### Dative (indirect object of the relative clause)

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Das ist der Mann, **dem** ich geholfen habe. | That is the man whom I helped. |
| Das ist die Frau, **der** ich das Buch gegeben habe. | That is the woman to whom I gave the book. |
| Das sind die Kinder, **denen** ich geschenkt habe. | Those are the children to whom I gave gifts. |

!!! warning "Dative adds -n to articles, but relative pronouns add -m"
    dem (masc/neut), der (fem), denen (plural). Not den - that is Accusative.

---

## 3. wo - for places and times

wo replaces a preposition + place/time when the meaning is clear:

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Das ist das Haus, **wo** ich wohne. | That is the house where I live. |
| Das ist die Stadt, **wo** ich geboren bin. | That is the city where I was born. |
| Das ist der Tag, **wo** ich ankomme. | That is the day when I arrive. |

!!! tip "wo is informal, preposition + der/die/das is formal"
    In speaking, wo is fine. In writing, prefer: Das ist das Haus, **in dem** ich wohne.

---

## 4. was - for things (after indefinite pronouns)

was replaces was/etwas/nichts/alles in relative clauses:

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Das ist alles, **was** ich weiß. | That is everything that I know. |
| Ich habe etwas, **was** ich dir geben muss. | I have something that I must give you. |
| Das ist nichts, **was** mich interessiert. | That is nothing that interests me. |

---

## 5. Word order in relative clauses

The verb goes to the END, same as all other Nebensatz connectors:

| Example | English |
| --- | --- |
| Der Mann, **der** dort **steht**, ist mein Vater. | The man who stands there is my father. |
| Das Buch, **das** ich **lese**, ist interessant. | The book that I read is interesting. |
| Die Frau, **der** ich **geholfen habe**, ist nett. | The woman whom I helped is nice. |

!!! tip "One rule"
    Relative clause = Nebensatz = verb at END. Same as weil, dass, wenn, etc.

## 7. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nom (who is subject) | der/die/das/die | Der Mann, der steht, ist nett. | The man who stands is nice. |
| Akk (who is object) | den/die/das/die | Der Mann, den ich kenne, ist nett. | The man whom I know is nice. |
| Dat (to whom) | dem/der/dem/denen | Der Mann, dem ich half, ist nett. | The man whom I helped is nice. |
| wo (place/time) | wo | Das ist das Haus, wo ich wohne. | That is the house where I live. |
| was (things) | was | Das ist alles, was ich weiß. | That is all that I know. |

- [ ] Relative pronoun matches the gender of the noun.
- [ ] Case changes the form: Nom/Akk/Dat.
- [ ] Verb goes to END (Nebensatz rule).
- [ ] wo for places, was for things.
