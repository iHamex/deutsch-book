# man and Word Order - A2 Deep (A2)

> At A1 you learned man (one/you) and basic word order (V2, verb at end after connectors). At A2 you use man with past tenses, learn the full adverb order (TEKAMOLO), and understand why adverbs go where they go.

---

## 1. man - the impersonal subject

man replaces a general "one" or "you" (people in general). It is always the subject, always Nominative, always 3rd person singular:

| Tense | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Präsens | **Man** spricht hier Deutsch. | One speaks German here. |
| Perfekt | **Man** hat viel **gegessen**. | A lot was eaten. (People ate a lot.) |
| Präteritum | **Man** ging ins Kino. | People went to the cinema. |
| Konjunktiv II | **Man** würde nicht **angenommen**. | One would not be accepted. |

!!! warning "man is always subject"
    Man is always Nominative. The verb is always 3rd person singular. Never change the verb form.

!!! tip "man vs Passive"
    Man spricht hier Deutsch. = Hier wird Deutsch gesprochen. (Same meaning, different structure.)

---

## 2. TEKAMOLO - the adverb order

German adverbs follow a fixed order. Think **TEKAMOLO**:

| Letter | Category | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **T** | Temporal | when | heute, morgen, gestern, um 8 Uhr, am Montag |
| **E** | Kausal | why | deshalb, deswegen, wegen des Regens |
| **K** | Konditional | under what condition | wenn möglich, bei gutem Wetter |
| **A** | Modal | how | gern, schnell, oft, manchmal |
| **M** | Lokal | where | in München, auf dem Tisch, nach Hause |
| **O** | Directional | where to | nach Berlin, ins Kino, zum Arzt |
| **L** | Lokal (final) | where (static) | da, hier, dort |

### Simple version: Time - Manner - Place (TMP)

For A2, remember **Time - Manner - Place**:

| Example | Time | Manner | Place | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ich arbeite | **montags** | **gern** | **im Büro**. | I like working in the office on Mondays. |
| Er fährt | **um 8 Uhr** | **mit dem Zug** | **nach Berlin**. | He drives to Berlin by train at 8. |
| Wir treffen uns | **morgen** | **gemeinsam** | **am Bahnhof**. | We meet at the station together tomorrow. |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Ich arbeite montags gern im Büro.* / *Er fährt um 8 Uhr mit dem Zug nach Berlin.*

## 3. Adverb position in the sentence

Adverbs go AFTER the verb and object, but BEFORE the infinitive/partizip at the end:

| Position | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject + Verb + **Adverb** + Object + End | Ich trinke **gerne** Kaffee. | I drink coffee gladly. |
| Subject + Verb + Object + **Adverb** + End | Ich trinke **gerne** Kaffee. | I like drinking coffee. |
| Subject + Verb + Time + Manner + Place + End | Ich arbeite **montags gern im Büro**. | I like working in the office on Mondays. |

### With modal verbs and Perfekt

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modal + Subject + ... + Adverb + Infinitiv | Ich **muss** heute **früh** **arbeiten**. | I have to work early today. |
| Helper + Subject + ... + Adverb + Partizip | Ich **habe** gestern **viel** **gegessen**. | I ate a lot yesterday. |

---

## 4. man + past tenses (common patterns)

| Pattern | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| man + Perfekt | Man hat viel **gegessen**. | People ate a lot. |
| man + Präteritum | Man **ging** ins Kino. | People went to the cinema. |
| man + modal | Man **kann** hier gut **essen**. | You can eat well here. |
| man + passive | Man **spricht** hier Deutsch. = Hier **wird** Deutsch **gesprochen**. | German is spoken here. |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Man hat viel gelacht.* (People laughed a lot.) / *Man geht hier nicht raus.* (You don't go out here.)

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| man (general) | 3rd person singular, always Nom | Man spricht Deutsch. | One speaks German. |
| TEKAMOLO | Time - Manner - Place | Ich arbeite montags gern im Büro. | I like working in the office on Mondays. |
| adverb position | after verb/object, before end | Ich trinke gern Kaffee. | I like drinking coffee. |
| man + Perfekt | man + helper + Partizip | Man hat viel gelacht. | People laughed a lot. |

## 7. Cheat sheet

| Pattern | Rule | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **man** | impersonal "one/you/people" | **Man** spricht hier Deutsch. | People speak German here. |
| **man + 3rd person** | verb like er/sie/es | Hier **kauft man** Tickets. | Here you buy tickets. |
| **TEKAMOLO** | Tempus-Kausal-Modal-Lokal | Ich fahre **heute wegen der Arbeit mit dem Zug nach München**. | time, reason, manner, place |
| **time before place** | when, then where | Ich lerne **am Montag in der Schule**. | on Monday at school |

- [ ] man + verb in 3rd person singular.
- [ ] Word order: time → reason → manner → place (TEKAMOLO).
- [ ] Never: place before time.
