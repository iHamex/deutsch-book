# Reason and Purpose - Why You Do Things (A2)

> At A1 you learned weil (verb at END) and damit (so that). At A2 you add denn (verb at POSITION 2), um-zu (in order to), ohne-zu (without doing), and anstatt-zu (instead of doing).

---

## 1. denn vs weil - same meaning, different word order

| Connector | Meaning | Word order | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **weil** | because | verb at END (Nebensatz) | Ich bleibe zu Hause, **weil** es regnet. | I stay home because it rains. |
| **denn** | because | verb at POSITION 2 (Hauptsatz) | Ich bleibe zu Hause, **denn** es regnet. | I stay home because it rains. |

!!! warning "weil vs denn - never mix word order"
    weil: verb at END. denn: verb at POSITION 2. Same meaning, completely different grammar.

| Wrong | Correct | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ich bleibe, weil **regnet es**. | Ich bleibe, weil es regnet. | weil sends verb to end |
| Ich bleibe, denn **regnet es**. | Ich bleibe, denn **es regnet**. | denn keeps V2 |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil es regnet.* / *Ich bleibe zu Hause, denn es regnet.*

---

## 2. damit - so that (different subject in both parts)

damit sends the verb to the END. It is used when the purpose has a DIFFERENT subject than the main clause:

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ..., damit + subject + verb at END | Ich lerne Deutsch, **damit** ich einen Job **finde**. | I learn German so that I find a job. |
| ..., damit + subject + verb at END | Er gibt mir Geld, **damit** ich das Ticket **kaufe**. | He gives me money so that I buy the ticket. |

Compare with um-zu (same subject, see section 3):

- Ich lerne Deutsch, **damit** ich einen Job **finde**. (damit - could have different subject)
- Ich lerne Deutsch, **um** einen Job **zu finden**. (um-zu - same subject, shorter)

---

## 3. um-zu - in order to (same subject in both parts)

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ..., um + zu + Infinitiv | Ich lerne Deutsch, **um** in Deutschland **zu arbeiten**. | I learn German in order to work in Germany. |
| ..., um + zu + Infinitiv | Er fährt nach Berlin, **um** seinen Freund **zu besuchen**. | He drives to Berlin in order to visit his friend. |

!!! tip "um-zu is the same-subject version of damit"
    Same subject in both parts = um-zu (shorter, more natural). Different subject = damit (must use damit).

---

## 4. ohne-zu - without doing

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ..., ohne + zu + Infinitiv | Er geht ins Kino, **ohne** mich **zu fragen**. | He goes to the cinema without asking me. |
| ..., ohne + zu + Infinitiv | Sie trinkt Kaffee, **ohne** Zucker **zu nehmen**. | She drinks coffee without taking sugar. |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Er geht ins Kino, ohne mich zu fragen.* (He goes to the cinema without asking me.)

---

## 5. anstatt-zu - instead of doing

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ..., anstatt + zu + Infinitiv | Er bleibt zu Hause, **anstatt** zu arbeiten. | He stays home instead of working. |
| ..., anstatt + zu + Infinitiv | Sie kauft online, **anstatt** ins Geschäft **zu gehen**. | She buys online instead of going to the shop. |

---

## 6. The purpose family - one table

| Connector | Meaning | Same subject? | Verb position | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **damit** | so that | different | verb at END | Ich lerne, damit ich einen Job finde. |
| **um...zu** | in order to | same | zu + Infinitiv | Ich lerne, um einen Job zu finden. |
| **ohne...zu** | without doing | same | zu + Infinitiv | Er geht, ohne mich zu fragen. |
| **anstatt...zu** | instead of doing | same | zu + Infinitiv | Sie bleibt, statt zu arbeiten. |

!!! warning "damit vs um-zu"
    Same subject in both parts? Use um-zu (shorter). Different subject? Use damit (must).

## 7. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| weil | because (verb at END) | Ich bleibe, weil es regnet. | I stay because it rains. |
| denn | because (verb at V2) | Ich bleibe, denn es regnet. | I stay because it rains. |
| damit | so that (different subject) | Ich lerne, damit ich einen Job finde. | I learn so that I find a job. |
| um-zu | in order to (same subject) | Ich lerne, um einen Job zu finden. | I learn in order to find a job. |
| ohne-zu | without doing | Er geht, ohne mich zu fragen. | He goes without asking me. |
| anstatt-zu | instead of doing | Sie bleibt, statt zu arbeiten. | She stays instead of working. |

- [ ] weil = verb at END. denn = verb at V2.
- [ ] damit = different subject. um-zu = same subject.
- [ ] ohne-zu and anstatt-zu follow the same pattern as um-zu.

## 8. Practice - reason and purpose

- I stay at home because it rains. (weil)
- I stay at home because it rains. (denn)
- I learn German so that I find a job. (different subject -> damit)
- He gives me money so that I buy the ticket. (damit)
- I learn German in order to work in Germany. (um-zu, same subject)
- He drives to Berlin in order to visit his friend. (um-zu)
- He goes to the cinema without asking me. (ohne-zu)
- She drinks coffee without taking sugar. (ohne-zu)
- He stays at home instead of working. (anstatt-zu)
- She buys online instead of going to the shop. (anstatt-zu)

## 9. Cheat sheet

| Connector | Same subject? | Word order | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **weil** | doesn't matter | verb at END | Ich bleibe zu Hause, **weil** es regnet. | because (reason) |
| **denn** | doesn't matter | verb stays at 2 | Ich bleibe zu Hause, **denn** es regnet. | because (same meaning, different order) |
| **damit** | different subject | verb at END | Ich kaufe ein Ticket, **damit** wir reisen können. | so that (two people) |
| **um ... zu** | same subject | zu + infinitive at END | Ich lerne Deutsch, **um** in Deutschland **zu arbeiten**. | in order to (one person) |
| **ohne ... zu** | same subject | zu + infinitive at END | Er geht, **ohne** etwas **zu sagen**. | without doing |
| **statt ... zu** | same subject | zu + infinitive at END | **Statt** zu arbeiten, schläft er. | instead of doing |

- [ ] weil + verb at END / denn + verb at position 2.
- [ ] um-zu = same subject; damit = different subject.
- [ ] ohne-zu / statt-zu always zu + infinitive.
