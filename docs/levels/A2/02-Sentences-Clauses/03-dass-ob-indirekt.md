# dass, ob, and Indirect Questions (A2)

> At A1 you learned dass (that) and ob (whether). At A2 you add indirect W-questions (Ich weiß nicht, wo er wohnt) and connect them all into opinions and reports.

---

## 1. dass - that (reporting a fact)

dass sends the verb to the END. It introduces a fact that someone believes or says:

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ..., dass + verb at END | Ich glaube, **dass** er **kommt**. | I believe that he is coming. |
| ..., dass + verb at END | Sie sagt, **dass** das Buch **teuer** ist. | She says that the book is expensive. |
| ..., dass + verb at END | Ich finde, **dass** Deutsch **schwer** ist. | I think that German is hard. |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Ich finde, dass Deutsch schwer ist.* (I think that German is hard.)

---

## 2. ob - whether / if (reporting a question)

ob also sends the verb to the END. It introduces a yes/no question that someone asked:

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ..., ob + verb at END | Ich frage mich, **ob** er **kommt**. | I wonder whether he is coming. |
| ..., ob + verb at END | Weißt du, **ob** der Laden **offen** ist? | Do you know whether the shop is open? |
| ..., ob + verb at END | Er fragte, **ob** ich Zeit **habe**. | He asked whether I have time. |

!!! warning "dass vs ob - never mix"
    dass = reporting a FACT (I know it). ob = reporting a QUESTION (I don't know it).
    - Ich weiß, **dass** er kommt. (I know he comes.)
    - Ich weiß nicht, **ob** er kommt. (I don't know if he comes.)

---

## 3. Indirect W-questions (indirekte W-Fragen)

When you report a W-question, the W-word stays, but the verb goes to the END:

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ..., W-word + verb at END | Ich weiß nicht, **wo** er **wohnt**. | I don't know where he lives. |
| ..., W-word + verb at END | Weißt du, **wann** der Zug **fährt**? | Do you know when the train leaves? |
| ..., W-word + verb at END | Er fragte, **wie** man zum Bahnhof **kommt**. | He asked how one gets to the station. |
| ..., W-word + verb at END | Sag mir, **was** du **willst**. | Tell me what you want. |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Ich weiß nicht, wo er wohnt.* / *Weißt du, wann der Zug fährt?*

## 4. Full list - what can introduce a Nebensatz

| Connector | Type | Verb position | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| als | time | END | Als ich kam, ging ich. |
| wenn | time/condition | END | Wenn es regnet, bleibe ich. |
| weil | reason | END | Weil ich müde bin, gehe ich. |
| dass | fact | END | Ich finde, dass es gut ist. |
| ob | question | END | Ich frag mich, ob er kommt. |
| damit | purpose | END | Ich lerne, damit ich arbeiten kann. |
| bevor | time (before) | END | Bevor ich gehe, rufe ich an. |
| nachdem | time (after) | END | Nachdem ich gegessen hatte, ging ich. |
| wo | place question | END | Ich weiß nicht, wo er wohnt. |
| wann | time question | END | Weißt du, wann es anfängt? |
| wie | manner question | END | Er fragte, wie man das macht. |
| was | thing question | END | Sag mir, was du willst. |
| wer | person question | END | Ich weiß nicht, wer das ist. |

!!! warning "One rule for ALL"
    After ANY of these connectors: verb goes to END. No exceptions.

## 5. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dass | fact (verb at END) | Ich finde, dass es gut ist. | I think that it is good. |
| ob | yes/no question (verb at END) | Ich frage mich, ob er kommt. | I wonder if he is coming. |
| indirect W-question | W-word + verb at END | Ich weiß nicht, wo er wohnt. | I don't know where he lives. |
| ob vs dass | ob = unknown, dass = known | Ich weiß, dass er kommt. / Ich weiß nicht, ob er kommt. | I know he comes. / I don't know if he comes. |

- [ ] dass = reporting a fact.
- [ ] ob = reporting a yes/no question.
- [ ] Indirect W-questions keep the W-word, verb goes to END.
- [ ] All Nebensatz connectors send verb to END.

## 7. Cheat sheet

| Introducer | Reports | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **dass** | a fact | Ich denke, **dass** er kommt. | I think that he is coming. |
| **ob** | a yes/no question | Ich weiß nicht, **ob** er kommt. | I don't know whether he is coming. |
| **W-word** | a W-question | Weißt du, **wann** er kommt? | Do you know when he is coming? |

- [ ] dass/ob/W-word send the verb to the END.
- [ ] ob = yes/no question inside the sentence (never wenn here!).
- [ ] Question word order inside: W-word + subject + ... + verb at END.
