# Opinion Connectors - Giving Reasons (A2)

> At A1 you knew aber, oder, denn, sondern, und. At A2 you add deshalb, deswegen, trotzdem, dann, trotz, statt, and you learn to give opinions in 2-3 sentences with reasons.

---

## 1. Coordinating connectors - verb stays at V2

These connectors join two independent clauses. Both clauses keep V2:

| Connector | Meaning | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **und** | and | Ich lerne Deutsch **und** ich arbeite. | I learn German and I work. |
| **oder** | or | Kommst du **oder** bleibst du? | Are you coming or are you staying? |
| **aber** | but | Ich bin müde, **aber** ich arbeite. | I am tired but I work. |
| **denn** | because | Ich gehe, **denn** es regnet. | I go because it rains. |
| **sondern** | but rather (after negation) | Ich trinke nicht Kaffee, **sondern** Tee. | I don't drink coffee but rather tea. |

---

## 2. Adverbial connectors - verb stays at V2 (position 1)

These connectors occupy position 1, so the verb goes to position 2 and the subject follows:

| Connector | Meaning | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **deshalb** | therefore | Es regnet, **deshalb** bleibe ich zu Hause. | It rains, therefore I stay home. |
| **deswegen** | therefore | Er ist krank, **deswegen** kommt er nicht. | He is sick, therefore he doesn't come. |
| **trotzdem** | nevertheless | Es regnet, **trotzdem** gehe ich raus. | It rains, nevertheless I go out. |
| **dann** | then | Ich koche, **dann** esse ich. | I cook, then I eat. |
| **also** | so | Er ist müde, **also** geht er früh ins Bett. | He is tired, so he goes to bed early. |
| **außerdem** | besides / moreover | Das Buch ist gut, **außerdem** ist es billig. | The book is good, besides it is cheap. |

!!! warning "deshalb vs trotzdem - opposite meaning"
    deshalb/deswegen = therefore (same direction as the reason). trotzdem = nevertheless (opposite direction to the reason).
    - Es regnet, **deshalb** bleibe ich zu Hause. (rain -> stay home, same direction)
    - Es regnet, **trotzdem** gehe ich raus. (rain -> go out, opposite direction)

## 3. Preposition connectors - verb goes to END

These behave like Nebensatz connectors:

| Connector | Meaning | Verb position | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **trotz** | despite (+ Gen, Dat in speech) | verb at END | **Trotz** des Regens **gehe** ich raus. | Despite the rain I go out. |
| **statt** | instead of (+ Gen or zu + Inf) | verb at END or zu + Inf | **Statt** zu arbeiten **bleibt** er zu Hause. | Instead of working he stays home. |
| **wegen** | because of (+ Dat in speech) | verb at END | **Wegen** des Regens **bleibe** ich zu Hause. | Because of the rain I stay home. |

### obwohl - although (receptive-active, the subordinating trotzdem)

**obwohl** = although. It expresses the same "against the expectation" idea as trotzdem, but as a Nebensatz connector: the verb goes to the END of the obwohl-clause.

| Connector | Word order | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **trotzdem** | V2 main clause | Es regnet, **trotzdem** gehe ich raus. | It rains, nevertheless I go out. |
| **obwohl** | verb at END | Ich gehe raus, **obwohl** es regnet. | I go out although it is raining. |
| **obwohl** | verb at END | Er kommt, **obwohl** er müde ist. | He comes although he is tired. |

- Ich gehe raus, **obwohl** es regnet. = Es regnet, **trotzdem** gehe ich raus.
- Er kommt, **obwohl** er krank ist. = Er ist krank, **trotzdem** kommt er.

!!! warning "trotzdem keeps V2, obwohl sends the verb to the END"
    Same meaning, different word order. Never write ~~... obwohl es regnet es~~ - after obwohl the verb goes to the END like after weil and dass.

!!! tip "Receptive at A2"
    Recognize obwohl in Lesen and Hören (it signals: the next clause goes against the expectation). Produce it only if it comes easily - trotzdem is your A2 speaking default.

## 4. Opinion phrases - say what you think

| Phrase | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ich finde, dass ... | Ich **finde**, dass Deutsch schwer ist. | I think that German is hard. |
| Meiner Meinung nach ... | **Meiner Meinung nach** ist das gut. | In my opinion that is good. |
| Ich glaube, dass ... | Ich **glaube**, dass er kommt. | I believe that he is coming. |
| Ich denke, dass ... | Ich **denke**, dass das richtig ist. | I think that is correct. |
| Ich habe den Eindruck, dass ... | Ich **habe den Eindruck**, dass es besser wird. | I have the impression that it is getting better. |

### Giving reasons (the pattern)

| Pattern | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ich finde ..., **denn** ... | Ich finde das gut, **denn** es ist praktisch. | I think it is good because it is practical. |
| Ich finde ..., **deshalb** ... | Das ist teuer, **deshalb** kaufe ich es nicht. | That is expensive, therefore I don't buy it. |
| ... **trotzdem** ... | Es ist teuer, **trotzdem** kaufe ich es. | It is expensive, nevertheless I buy it. |

!!! tip "Opinions need reasons"
    At A2, never give an opinion alone. Always add a reason: Ich finde das gut, **denn** es ist praktisch. One sentence = incomplete.

## 5. Practice - connect your opinion

Join each idea with the right connector (deshalb, trotzdem, denn, wegen, trotz, sondern, außerdem).

- It rains, therefore I stay at home. (deshalb)
- It rains, nevertheless I go out. (trotzdem)
- He is sick, therefore he does not come. (deswegen)
- I think that is good because it is practical. (denn)
- That is expensive, therefore I do not buy it. (deshalb)
- It is expensive, nevertheless I buy it. (trotzdem)
- I do not drink coffee but rather tea. (sondern)
- Despite the rain I go out. (trotz)
- Because of the rain I stay at home. (wegen)
- Instead of working he stays at home. (statt)
- The book is good, besides it is cheap. (außerdem)
- He is tired, so he goes to bed early. (also)
- I cook, then I eat. (dann)

## 6. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| therefore | deshalb/deswegen (V1, verb V2) | Es regnet, deshalb bleibe ich. | It rains, therefore I stay. |
| nevertheless | trotzdem (V1, verb V2) | Es regnet, trotzdem gehe ich. | It rains, nevertheless I go. |
| because of | wegen + Dat (verb END) | Wegen des Regens bleibe ich. | Because of the rain I stay. |
| despite | trotz + Gen (verb END) | Trotz des Regens gehe ich. | Despite the rain I go. |
| opinion | Ich finde, dass ... | Ich finde, dass es gut ist. | I think it is good. |
| reason | ... denn/deshalb ... | Ich finde es gut, denn es ist praktisch. | I think it is good because it is practical. |

- [ ] deshalb/trotzdem = position 1, verb V2.
- [ ] wegen/trotz = prepositions, verb at END.
- [ ] Opinions always need reasons (denn/deshalb).
