# Comparative and Superlative - Full (A2)

> At A1 you learned the basics: adj + er, am + adj +sten. At A2 you master irregulars (gut-besser-am besten), als vs wie, and the receptive je...desto construction.

---

## 1. Regular comparative and superlative

| Rule | Example | Comparative | Superlative | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| most adjectives: stem + **-er** | schnell | **schneller** | am **schnellsten** | fast - faster - fastest |
| a/o/u take umlaut where possible | alt | **älter** | am **ältesten** | old - older - oldest |
| adjectives ending in -el/-er: drop the -e- before -er | dunkel, teuer | **dunkler**, **teurer** | am **dunkelsten**, am **teuersten** | dark, expensive |
| adjectives ending in -d/-t/-s/-z: -est in the superlative | nett, groß | **netter**, **größer** | am **nettesten**, am **größten** | nice, big |

## 2. Irregular comparatives (memorize these)

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gut | **besser** | am **besten** | good - better - best |
| viel | **mehr** | am **meisten** | much - more - most |
| gern | **lieber** | am **liebsten** | gladly - more gladly - most gladly |
| nah | **näher** | am **nächsten** | near - nearer - nearest |
| hoch | **höher** | am **höchsten** | high - higher - highest |
| weit | **weiter** | am **weitesten** | far - further - furthest |

!!! warning "gut/besser/besten - never say guther"
    gut has no regular form. Never *guter. Only besser, besten.

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Besser als gestern.* (Better than yesterday.) / *Am besten geht es mir.* (I feel best.)

---

## 3. als vs wie - comparison

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **adj + als** (comparing things) | Er ist **größer als** sie. | He is taller than she. |
| **so + adj + wie** (same comparison) | Er ist **so groß wie** sein Bruder. | He is as tall as his brother. |
| **nicht so + adj + wie** (not as ... as) | Das ist **nicht so gut wie** das andere. | That is not as good as the other. |

!!! warning "als vs wie - never mix"
    als = comparison (bigger THAN). wie = equality (AS ... AS).
    - Er ist größer **als** sie. (bigger than)
    - Er ist so groß **wie** sie. (as tall as)

## 4. Superlative in sentences

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **am + adj +sten** (predicate) | Das ist **am billigsten**. | That is the cheapest. |
| **am + adj +sten** (predicate) | Am besten gefällt mir das blaue Auto. | I like the blue car best. |
| **der/die/das + adj +ste** (attribute) | Das **größte** Problem ist Geld. | The biggest problem is money. |
| **am + adj +sten** (adverb) | Er fährt **am schnellsten**. | He drives the fastest. |

!!! tip "Chunk to memorize"
    *Am besten gefällt mir das blaue Auto.* (I like the blue car best.) / *Das größte Problem ist Geld.* (The biggest problem is money.)

## 5. je...desto - the receptive construction

je...desto means "the more...the more". Both sides use comparative:

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| je + comparative ... desto + comparative | **Je** mehr ich lerne, **desto** besser werde ich. | The more I learn, the better I become. |
| je + comparative ... desto + comparative | **Je** schneller ich fahre, **desto** gefährlicher wird es. | The faster I drive, the more dangerous it becomes. |

!!! tip "Receptive"
    je...desto is receptive-active at A2. Recognize it in Lesen, but you don't need to produce it in Sprechen.

## 6. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| regular | adj + er / am + adj +sten | schneller / am schnellsten | faster / fastest |
| irregular | memorize gut-besser-besten, viel-mehr-meisten | besser / am besten | better / best |
| comparison | adj + als | Er ist größer als sie. | He is taller than she. |
| equality | so + adj + wie | Er ist so groß wie sie. | He is as tall as she. |
| superlative (predicate) | am + adj +sten | Das ist am billigsten. | That is the cheapest. |
| superlative (attribute) | der/die/das + adj +ste | Das größte Problem | The biggest problem |
| je...desto | the more...the more | Je mehr ich lerne, desto besser werde ich. | The more I learn, the better I become. |

- [ ] a/o/u take umlaut in comparative.
- [ ] gut/besser/besten, viel-mehr-meisten, gern-lieber-liebsten.
- [ ] als = comparison. wie = equality.
- [ ] je...desto = the more...the more (receptive).

## 8. Cheat sheet

| Form | Rule | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| comparative | adjective + -er (umlaut a/o/u) | alt -> **älter** | older |
| superlative | am + -sten / der -ste | am **ältesten** / der **älteste** | oldest |
| irregular | gut/besser/am besten, viel/mehr/am meisten | Das ist **besser**. | better |
| **als** | comparing two things | Ich bin größer **als** du. | than |
| **wie** | equality | Er ist so groß **wie** ich. | as ... as |
| **je ... desto** | the more ... the more (receptive) | **Je** mehr, **desto** besser. | the more, the better |

- [ ] comparative + als (not wie!); equality + wie.
- [ ] umlaut in short adjectives: alt/älter, groß/größer.
- [ ] je...desto = receptive only at A2.
