## What you can do after this Part

- Produce 10 past-tense sentences and 10 Nebensätze from memory without notes.
- Plan an activity with a partner using 5 planning skeletons.
- Keep the A2 mistake log empty of open items.

## Tip

One skeleton per conversation. Match the situation to the skeleton first, then fill exactly one slot. Do not translate word by word.
