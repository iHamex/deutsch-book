# telc A2 Exam Cheat Sheet

> The whole exam on one page. Read this the night before, then sleep.

---

## The exam at a glance

| Part | Teile | Duration | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hören** (Listening) | 3 Teile | ca. 20 min | Telefonansagen, Radio-Infos, Gespräch. Write keywords. |
| **Lesen** (Reading) | 3 Teile | 50 min total (shared with Schreiben) | Information anwenden, richtig/falsch, Anzeigen zuordnen. |
| **Schreiben** (Writing) | 2 Aufgaben | (same 50 min) | Formular + Brief (ca. 40 Wörter, 3 Punkte wählen). |
| **Sprechen** (Speaking) | 3 Teile | ca. 15 min | Vorstellen, Alltag, gemeinsam planen. Zu zweit. |

**Scoring: 60 points total. Pass = 36 points. No per-part minimum.**

---

## Hören - Listening

### Teil 1 - Telefonansagen + Notizen
- Listen for: names, numbers, times, addresses.
- Write DIGITS, not words (8:00, not "acht Uhr").
- Listen for KEY words: Termin, Montag, Nummer.

### Teil 2 - Radio-Infos / Ansagen
- Listen for: where, when, what, who.
- Match each announcement to the right picture/statement.
- Listen TWICE. Write during first play, check during second.

### Teil 3 - Längeres Gespräch
- Listen for: opinions, decisions, reasons.
- Mark "richtig" only if the text says it exactly.
- "Immer" and "nie" are usually traps.

!!! danger "Exam trap: digits"
    Write numbers as digits (8, 15, 200). Never write "acht" or "zwanzig". Digits are faster and risk-free.

---

## Lesen - Reading

### Teil 1 - Information anwenden
- Read the sign/text, then match to the situation.
- One key word is usually enough.
- Match: WHO does WHAT WHERE.

### Teil 2 - Artikel richtig/falsch
- Read the statement first, then scan the text.
- "Richtig" = the text says exactly the same.
- "Falsch" = the text says the opposite or nothing about it.
- Watch: immer, nie, nur, alle, kein - these change everything.

### Teil 3 - Anzeigen zuordnen
- Match each ad to the right person/situation.
- Read the person's needs, then scan the ads.
- One keyword match is usually enough.

!!! danger "Exam trap: richtig/falsch"
    "Richtig" means the text confirms it exactly. If the text doesn't mention it, it's NOT richtig. When in doubt, mark falsch.

## Schreiben - Writing

### Aufgabe 1 - Formular ausfüllen
- Fill every box. Never leave a box empty.
- Write what is asked, nothing more.
- Use digits for numbers.

### Aufgabe 2 - Brief (ca. 40 Wörter)
- Choose 3 of 4 Punkte.
- Write: Anrede + 2-3 sentences + Gruß.
- Use: Ich finde..., denn..., deshalb/trotzdem.

### Brief-Template (copy this)

> Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,
>
> ich schreibe wegen [Punkt 1]. [Punkt 2]. [Punkt 3].
>
> Mit freundlichen Grüßen
> [Name]

!!! danger "Exam rule: never empty"
    A guess can be right. An empty box is always 0 points. ALWAYS write something.

## Sprechen - Speaking (zu zweit, keine Vorbereitungszeit)

### Teil 1 - Sich vorstellen (1-2 min)
- Who are you? Where are you from? What do you do?
- Say 4-5 sentences. Use: Ich komme aus..., ich wohne in..., ich arbeite bei..., mein Hobby ist...

### Teil 2 - Alltagsgespräch (3-4 min)
- Ask AND answer questions about a topic.
- Give opinions with reasons: Ich finde..., denn...
- Ask your partner: Was denken Sie? / Was machen Sie gern?

### Teil 3 - Gemeinsam planen (3-4 min)
- Discuss options with your partner.
- Make a decision together.
- Use: Was schlagen Sie vor? / Ich finde... / Stimmt, das machen wir.

!!! danger "Exam rule: speak, don't stay silent"
    If you don't know a word, say something else. Silence = 0 points. Every sentence = points.

## Quick practice - the exam trigger frames

Translate each frame. Say it out loud, then check. These are the exact situations the A2 exam throws at you.

- I am from Iran, from Tehran. (Teil 1)
- I have been learning German for two years. (Teil 1)
- I like to read and to travel. (Teil 1 hobbies)
- I would like to book a room. (form/shop)
- How much does the ticket cost? (shop)
- I have a complaint. (Reklamation)
- Could you please say that again? (didn't hear)
- Can I leave a message? (phone)
- I'll call again later. (phone)
- What do you think? (opinion)
- I think that is a good idea. (opinion)
- Why not? That suits me. (agreeing)
- I am sorry, but that does not work for me. (disagreeing)
- We could meet at 10 o'clock. (planning)
- Are you free on Saturday? (planning)
