## What you can do after this Part

- Finish Lesen + Schreiben inside the 50-minute block with time left.
- Catch Telefonansagen details (numbers, names, times) on first listen.
- Write a 40-word Brief covering 3 Punkte with correct Anrede and Gruß.
- Survive Sprechen zu zweit: vorstellen, Fragen stellen/beantworten, gemeinsam planen.

## Timed Practice (weekly, 45-55 min - full mock run)

1. Set a timer for 45-55 minutes and work through [08-timed-mock-test.md](08-timed-mock-test.md) in one sitting - mini Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen. This is a shortened practice sprint in the style of the real exam (the real written part is 70 minutes, the oral ~15).
2. Hören: 10 min (announcements + talk). Lesen: 15 min (ads + richtig/falsch). Schreiben: 15 min (Formular + one ca. 40-Wort Brief using the 3 Punkte = 3 Sätze frame). Sprechen: 10 min aloud (Teil 2/3 role-play card, both roles).
3. Score yourself against the hidden answers.

!!! warning "Self-score rule"
    Below 80% on any part? Re-do that part's drills in [08-Practice-Lab](../08-Practice-Lab/00-INDEX.md), then repeat the mock after 24 hours. Below 60% on the Brief? Rewrite it once a day until it passes.

- [ ] Full mock done in one timed sitting.
- [ ] All 4 parts self-scored.
- [ ] Re-test plan made for any part under 80%.

## Tip

Telc A2 is not modular: one weak part can be balanced by strong parts (only total counts), but a failed exam repeats WHOLE. Train the weakest part most.
