# Lesen Survival (telc A2)

> Lesen has 3 Teile in 50 minutes (shared with Schreiben). 30 points total. Train: scanning for keywords, traps (immer/nie/nur/alle), matching.

---

## Teil 1 - Information anwenden (match sign to situation)

### What happens
You see signs, notices, or short texts. Match each to the right situation/person.

### Strategy
1. Read the SITUATION first (what is the person doing?).
2. Scan the SIGNS for one matching keyword.
3. One keyword match is usually enough.

### Common signs

| Sign | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geöffnet von 9 bis 18 Uhr | Open from 9 to 6 | The shop is open during these hours. |
| Eingang verboten | No entry | You cannot go in. |
| Nur für Kunden | Customers only | Only customers can use this. |
| Gratis | Free | No cost. |
| Achtung! Baustelle | Attention! Construction site | Road works ahead. |
| Bitte nicht stören | Please do not disturb | Do not knock/enter. |
| Zutritt nur mit Ausweis | Access only with ID | You need your ID card. |
| Feuer! | Fire! | Emergency! |
| Ruhe bitte | Quiet please | Be quiet. |
| Ausgang | Exit | The way out. |

!!! tip "Scan for keywords"
    Don't read every word. Scan for the KEY word that matches the situation.

## Teil 2 - Artikel richtig/falsch (true/false)

### What happens
You read a statement, then check if the text confirms it (richtig) or contradicts it (falsch).

### Strategy
1. Read the STATEMENT first.
2. Find the relevant part of the TEXT.
3. Check: does the text say exactly the same?
4. Watch for: immer, nie, nur, alle, kein, immer.

### Trap words

| Trap | Example | Why it's a trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **immer** (always) | Er geht immer pünktlich. | One exception = falsch |
| **nie** (never) | Sie trinkt nie Kaffee. | One coffee = falsch |
| **nur** (only) | Er isst nur Salat. | One meat = falsch |
| **alle** (all) | Alle Kinder spielen draußen. | One inside = falsch |
| **kein** (no) | Er hat kein Auto. | One car = falsch |

!!! danger "richtig = text says it exactly"
    If the text doesn't mention it, it's NOT richtig. "Nicht erwähnt" = falsch.

## Teil 3 - Anzeigen zuordnen (match ads)

### What happens
You see ads and match each to the right person/situation.

### Strategy
1. Read the PERSON's needs first.
2. Scan each AD for one matching keyword.
3. One keyword match is usually enough.

### Common ad vocabulary

| German | English |
| --- | --- |
| Gesucht | Wanted/Sought |
| Angebot | Offer |
| Verkaufe | Selling |
| Kaufe | Buying |
| Suche | Looking for |
| Biete | Offering |
| Kostenlos / Gratis | Free |
| Nur für... | Only for... |
| Ab sofort | Immediately |
| Hinweis | Note/Information |

## Quick practice - the trap words

Read each short German phrase. Decide: does it say yes or no for the situation given? Say your answer in English, then check. Watch immer/nie/nur/alle.

- Situation: wants to park. Text: „Parken verboten.“ (can he park?)
- Situation: wants to enter. Text: „Betreten verboten.“ (can she enter?)
- Situation: needs a discount. Text: „Ermäßigung für Studenten.“ (does she get one?)
- Situation: wants photos. Text: „Fotos verboten.“ (may he take photos?)
- Situation: wants to smoke. Text: „Rauchen verboten.“ (may he smoke?)
- Situation: open now? Text: „geöffnet von 8 bis 18 Uhr“ - it is 17 Uhr. (open?)
- Situation: open now? Text: „geöffnet von 8 bis 18 Uhr“ - it is 19 Uhr. (open?)
- Situation: wants to buy shoes. Text: „Verkauf nur bis Samstag.“ (can she still buy on Monday?)

??? success "Antworten - expand to check"

    1. Nein - no parking.
    2. Nein - no entry.
    3. Ja - student discount.
    4. Nein - photos forbidden.
    5. Nein - smoking forbidden.
    6. Ja - it is still open (17 < 18).
    7. Nein - it is closed (19 > 18).
    8. Nein - sale ends Saturday.
