## What you can do after this Part

- Give opinions with reasons in 3 sentences without stopping.
- Survive any phone call: appointments, complaints, messages.
- Complain politely and get what you need (refund, repair, appointment).
- Tell a 1-minute past story with als and Präteritum.

## Timed Practice (weekly, 15 min)

1. Set a timer for 15 minutes.
2. Speak ONE model dialogue aloud TWICE - from [telefonieren.md](02-telefonieren.md) or [reklamation-beratung.md](03-reklamation-beratung.md). Take both roles.
3. Tell a 1-minute past story aloud - from [kleine-geschichten.md](05-kleine-geschichten.md) frame, with your own facts.
4. Give one 3-sentence opinion with a reason (Ich finde ... denn ... trotzdem ...).

!!! warning "Self-score rule"
    If you hesitated more than 3 times or stopped for more than 5 seconds, repeat the same dialogue after 24 hours. Fluency, not perfection.

- [ ] 1 dialogue spoken twice aloud.

- [ ] Opinion with reason in 3 sentences.

## Tip

A2 speaking = A1 chunks + denn/deshalb/trotzdem + one als-sentence. Opinions need reasons - never one sentence alone.
