## What you can do after this Part

- Give opinions with reasons in 3 sentences without stopping.
- Survive any phone call: appointments, complaints, messages.
- Complain politely and get what you need (refund, repair, appointment).
- Tell a 1-minute past story with als and Präteritum.

## Tip

A2 speaking = A1 chunks + denn/deshalb/trotzdem + one als-sentence. Opinions need reasons - never one sentence alone.
