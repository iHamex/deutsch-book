# Passive Alternatives (A2, Receptive)

> There are three ways to say the same thing without using passive. At A2 you recognize them all and can choose the right one.

---

## 1. Man-Sätze - the informal alternative

| Passive | Man-Satz | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hier wird Deutsch gesprochen. | Man spricht hier Deutsch. | German is spoken here. |
| Das Buch wird gelesen. | Man liest das Buch. | People read the book. |
| Das Auto wird repariert. | Man repariert das Auto. | People repair the car. |

!!! tip "Use man-Sätze in speech"
    Man-Sätze are easier to produce and more natural in everyday German. Save passive for writing and formal contexts.

---

## 2. -bar adjectives (receptive)

-bare means "able to be [verbed]" - like English "-able":

| Verb | -bar adjective | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| machen | **machbar** | Das ist machbar. | That is doable. |
| lesen | **lesbar** | Das ist lesbar. | That is readable. |
| essen | **essbar** | Das ist essbar. | That is edible. |
| trinken | **trinkbar** | Das ist trinkbar. | That is drinkable. |
| verstehen | **verstehbar** | Das ist verstehbar. | That is understandable. |
| hören | **hörbar** | Das ist hörbar. | That is audible. |
| ändern | **änderbar** | Das ist änderbar. | That is changeable. |
| schließen | **schließbar** | Das ist schließbar. | That is closable. |
| waschen | **waschbar** | Das ist waschbar. | That is washable. |

!!! tip "receptive"
    -bar adjectives are receptive-active. Recognize them in Lesen; you don't need to produce them in Sprechen.

---

## 3. sein + zu + Infinitiv (receptive)

This structure means "is to be [verbed]" or "can be [verbed]":

| Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sein + zu + Infinitiv | Das ist **zu machen**. | That is doable. |
| sein + zu + Infinitiv | Das ist **zu verstehen**. | That is understandable. |
| sein + zu + Infinitiv | Das ist **zu lesen**. | That is readable. |
| sein + zu + Infinitiv | Das ist **zu schwer zu verstehen**. | That is too hard to understand. |

!!! warning "receptive"
    sein + zu is receptive at A2. Recognize it in signs and formal text. Don't produce it in Sprechen.

---

## 4. Comparison - same meaning, three ways

| Passive | Man-Satz | -bar / sein + zu | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Das wird gemacht. | Man macht das. | Das ist machbar. | That is done. / People do that. / That is doable. |
| Das wird gelesen. | Man liest das. | Das ist lesbar. | That is read. / People read that. / That is readable. |
| Das wird verstanden. | Man versteht das. | Das ist verstehbar. | That is understood. / People understand that. / That is understandable. |

---

## 5. Cheat sheet

| Job | Form | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| man-Satz | informal passive | Man spricht hier Deutsch. | German is spoken here. |
| -bar | able to be [verbed] | Das ist machbar. | That is doable. |
| sein + zu | is to be [verbed] | Das ist zu verstehen. | That is understandable. |

- [ ] man-Sätze = informal, everyday German.
- [ ] -bar = -able (receptive).
- [ ] sein + zu = is to be (receptive).
- [ ] All three are alternatives to passive.

## 6. Practice - three ways, one meaning

- German is spoken here. (man-Satz)
- People read the book. (man-Satz)
- People repair the car. (man-Satz)
- That is doable. (-bar)
- That is readable. (-bar)
- That is understandable. (-bar)
- That is washable. (-bar)
- That is understandable. (sein + zu)
- That is too hard to understand. (sein + zu)
- That can be done. (Passive + kann - from the last lesson)

## 7. Cheat sheet

| Alternative | Structure | Example | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **man** + active verb | man + 3rd person | **Man** spricht hier Deutsch. | German is spoken here. |
| **-bar** adjective (receptive) | stem + -bar | Das ist **machbar**. | That is doable. |
| **sein + zu + Infinitiv** (receptive) | sein + zu + verb | Das ist **nicht zu ändern**. | That cannot be changed. |
| Passive | werden + Partizip II | Hier **wird** Deutsch **gesprochen**. | German is spoken here. |

**Same meaning, three ways:**

- Man öffnet die Tür. = Die Tür wird geöffnet. = Die Tür ist zu öffnen.

- [ ] man-Sätze = the everyday alternative (speak these).
- [ ] -bar and sein+zu = receptive (reading only).
- [ ] Never mix: sein + zu, not werden + zu.
